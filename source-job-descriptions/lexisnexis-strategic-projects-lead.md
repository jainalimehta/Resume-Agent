## About the Role

As a Strategy Specialist, you will support the development and execution of strategic initiatives that drive growth, profitability, and market success. Working closely with sales, marketing, and cross-functional stakeholders, you will analyze business opportunities, lead strategic projects, and provide insights that help shape go-to-market strategies and business decisions.

## Responsibilities

- Assist in developing short-, mid-, and long-term goals and strategic objectives.
- Develop, propose, and coordinate Sales and Marketing Strategy initiatives for different market segments.
- Use Salesforce.com to track strategy initiatives, activities, leads, opportunities, and sales.
- Identify, analyze, and monitor issues affecting profitability, growth, and productivity.
- Analyze market research to evaluate product and market requirements and create initiatives for new business opportunities.
- Lead and coordinate strategy projects across segments and divisions, including implementation plans and project timelines.
- Build and maintain stakeholder relationships, ensuring clear communication and engagement throughout projects.
- Acquire knowledge of internal and competitive offerings, products, pricing, and solutions.

## Requirements

- Experience related to the duties and responsibilities specified.
- Consulting experience.
- Advanced Microsoft Excel knowledge, including VLOOKUP, PivotTables, and charts and graphs.

- Inquisitive mindset and ability to translate data into actionable insights.
- Ability to create and deliver concise, informative presentations.
- Excellent oral, written, interpersonal, and communication skills.
- Highly organized, with strong time management and the ability to work autonomously.
