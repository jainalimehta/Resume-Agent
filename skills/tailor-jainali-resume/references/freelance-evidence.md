## Inclusion Rules

- Include freelance work as a standalone Experience entry only after Jainali supplies a defensible date range and at least one real engagement or deliverable relevant to the target role.
- Use `Independent Freelance Consultant` when combining multiple confirmed functions. Use `Freelance Developer`, `Freelance Architect`, or `Freelance Data Engineer` when one function is the clear target-role match.
- Client names may remain confidential. Use a truthful client type or industry only when Jainali supplies it, such as `small-business client` or `education-sector client`.
- Tailor bullet selection and terminology to the posting only when the underlying work supports it.
- A bullet may combine real tasks from the same freelance period when the wording remains defensible in an interview.
- Do not convert a personal, academic, or GitHub project into paid client work unless Jainali explicitly confirms that it was part of an engagement.
- Do not invent technologies, production use, metrics, stakeholder levels, team sizes, scope, or outcomes.

## Engagement Intake

For every engagement intended for a resume, record:

1. Approximate month/year or year range.
2. Function performed: developer, architect, data engineer, or a truthful combination.
3. Client name or confidential client type/industry.
4. Business or technical problem.
5. Actual tools and platforms used.
6. Work personally performed and level of ownership.
7. Deliverable provided.
8. How the deliverable was used.
9. Any measurable result Jainali can substantiate.
10. People collaborated with and their roles, if known.

## Interview-Defense Standard

For each resume bullet, Jainali must be able to explain the situation, task, actions, technical decisions, deliverable, and result without introducing facts absent from this ledger. If a follow-up answer would depend on guessing, narrow the bullet before submission.
