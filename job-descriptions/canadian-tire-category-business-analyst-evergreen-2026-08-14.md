## Role

The Category Business Analyst supports Canadian Tire's buying organization by managing vendor processes, examining new and existing product lines, and producing analysis that helps the company respond to changing customer needs.

The analyst supports Category Business Managers in creating relevant store assortments, supplying robust analysis and key insights that strengthen the brand, meet customer needs, and deliver category objectives and financial goals.

## Candidate profile

- Undergraduate or postgraduate degree in Business, Commerce, Retail Management, Data & Analytics, or a related discipline.
- Retail analytical experience is an asset; new graduates are welcome.
- Demonstrated collaboration, influence, and negotiation with colleagues or senior business members.
- Strong presentations, with style and format adapted to the audience.
