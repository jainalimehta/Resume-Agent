# BI Specialist / Data Analyst

**Company:** Tayco  
**Location:** Etobicoke, Ontario  
**Employment:** 12-month contract; one existing vacancy  
**Salary:** CAD $60,000–$65,000 annually  
**Work arrangement:** Based at Tayco's Etobicoke facility; flexible arrangement described as one office day per week after training

## Position Summary

The BI Specialist / Data Analyst turns data from Tayco's manufacturing and business systems into actionable management reporting. The position designs, builds, and maintains Power BI reports and data models, writes and tunes SQL, maintains Crystal Reports, and supports a small cross-functional team of two full-stack developers and a project coordinator. The role provides the business-intelligence layer above in-house web and software development and contributes reporting, communication, and design support.

## Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain Power BI reports, dashboards, datasets, and data models, including scheduled refreshes, gateway configuration, and workspace access.
- Write, review, and tune SQL queries, views, and stored procedures against ERP and in-house application databases.
- Develop and maintain Crystal Reports and support report consumers.
- Translate management requests into reporting requirements, definitions, and required detail; confirm definitions before building.
- Apply consistent visual design, visual hierarchy, and appropriate chart and table selection.
- Administer reporting infrastructure on Windows Server, including jobs, permissions, service accounts, file shares, and distribution.
- Contribute light HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap work and embed reports into internal web applications.
- Prepare management presentations, one-page summaries, and project status reporting.
- Use Git and GitHub for version control with branching and review practices.
- Reconcile reported figures to source systems and resolve discrepancies before reporting.
- Document sources, definitions, measures, and reporting processes.
- Train and support report consumers.
- Use AI-assisted tools with judgement and remain accountable for every query, measure, and figure.
- Perform other analysis and reporting work as required.

## Requirements

- Post-secondary education in business intelligence, computer science, information systems, statistics, or related field, or three to five years of relevant industry experience.
- Demonstrated Power BI proficiency, including data modelling, DAX, Power Query, report design, publishing, and scheduled refreshes.
- Strong SQL, including joins, aggregation, window functions, views, and query performance tuning.
- Crystal Reports development and maintenance experience.
- Reporting-related Windows Server administration knowledge.
- Working HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap knowledge for safe changes and embedded reporting.
- Git and GitHub version control, including branching, pull requests, and code review.
- Effective, accountable use of AI-assisted tools.
- Ability to gather requirements, present to non-technical audiences, validate figures, trace discrepancies, collaborate cross-functionally, prioritize work, and translate business requirements into technical applications.
- Strong analysis, problem-solving, communication, organization, attention to detail, initiative, adaptability, planning, and relationship building.

## Assets

- Tayco reporting environment: Power BI, Crystal Reports, SQL against ERP and PostgreSQL databases, Django internal applications, Windows Server, Git, and GitHub.
- SQL Server Reporting Services or Power BI Report Server.
- Data warehouse or star-schema design.
- Advanced Excel data modelling.
- Python or R, Azure data services, or manufacturing and ERP data across production, inventory, purchasing, and sales.

## Working Conditions and Benefits

Some travel, presentations, and overtime may be required. Benefits include flexible work arrangements after training, social and inclusion committees, and growth and development opportunities. Tayco may use AI to pre-screen applicants.
