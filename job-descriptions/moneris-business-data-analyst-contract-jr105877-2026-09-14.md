# Moneris — Business Data Analyst (Contract)

- Location: Toronto, Ontario (hybrid)
- Requisition: JR105877
- Contract: 6 months
- Reports to: Manager, Data Analytics
- Salary: CAD $71,000–$100,000

## Opportunity

Design, develop, maintain, and support reporting and analytical solutions that influence decision-making and business performance. Leverage modern data warehousing, transformation, and BI tools to deliver actionable insights supporting operations and strategic initiatives.

## Responsibilities

- Analyze and mine data to deliver financial insights, performance reporting, and recommendations.
- Lead and support moderately to highly complex analytical projects and ad hoc initiatives under management guidance.
- Design, develop, and maintain dashboards, scorecards, reports, and visualizations using Tableau or Power BI.
- Build and support data models, reporting datasets, and data-management solutions.
- Gather, document, and translate reporting requirements with business and technology stakeholders.
- Write and optimize SQL to extract, transform, and analyze data.
- Perform data profiling and validation across multiple sources and platforms.
- Develop knowledge of enterprise data assets, upstream sources, and reporting environments.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Business, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.
- 3–5 years working with relational databases and multiple data sources.
- SQL proficiency and relational database experience.
- Hands-on Tableau or Power BI experience and knowledge of visualization best practices.
- At least one year of dbt and Snowflake experience.
- Payments-industry experience is an asset.

Moneris encourages applicants to explain transferable experience. AI-enabled screening may be used, with outputs reviewed by recruiters.
