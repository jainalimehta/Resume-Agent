# AI Response Evaluator

**Company:** Mercor  
**Work arrangement:** Fully remote; independent contractor

## Overview

Evaluate AI-generated responses and provide structured written feedback. This opportunity is intended for analytical thinkers contributing to high-impact AI research projects.

## Basic Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree from a top-500 globally ranked university preferred.
- Strong analytical and written communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and follow detailed task guidelines.

## Required Skills

- Strong critical-reading skills, including identifying nuance, implicit meaning, and gaps in reasoning.
- Ability to write clear, precise, well-evidenced rationales that go beyond surface-level observations.
- Consistent and honest judgment, including critical assessments when warranted.
- Strict attention to detail and accurate application of structured evaluation guidelines.
- Ability to work entirely without AI writing tools.

## Eligibility

- Native English fluency required.

## Contract and Payment Terms

- Independent-contractor engagement.
- Fully remote work completed on the contractor's own schedule.
- Projects can be extended, shortened, or concluded early depending on project needs and performance.
- Work will not involve confidential or proprietary information from another employer, client, or institution.
- Weekly payments through Stripe or Wise based on services rendered.
- Mercor cannot support H1-B or STEM OPT candidates for this opportunity.

## About Mercor

Mercor partners with leading AI laboratories and enterprises to train frontier models using human expertise. Projects focus on training and enhancing AI systems. Contributors are paid competitively, collaborate with researchers, and help shape advanced AI systems in their areas of expertise.

Mercor considers qualified applicants without regard to legally protected characteristics and provides reasonable accommodations upon request.
