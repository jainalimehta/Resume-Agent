## The Adventure Ahead

As a Data Analyst within the Product Engineering family, you will transform raw information into the strategic compass that guides the organization. Reporting to the Manager of Data Analytics, your mission is to leverage company-wide data to generate actionable insights and build visualizations that enhance performance across the business. You will collaborate with the Data team, contribute to core data-driven decision-making, and ensure data accuracy and reliability.

Docebo is looking for an analytical thinker who goes beyond simple request fulfillment—someone who actively interprets data, challenges assumptions, and turns complex datasets into compelling, actionable business insights.

## The Day-to-Day

- Manipulate and analyze complex datasets to uncover actionable insights and support key business decisions.
- Design and deploy interactive dashboards using advanced data visualization tools to track performance metrics.
- Partner with the Data team and cross-functional teams to understand business requirements and deliver tailored analytical solutions.
- Conduct predictive modelling, correlation analysis, and statistical evaluations to identify trends and opportunities.
- Implement data validation and governance processes to maintain data accuracy and reliability.
- Reverse engineer data requirements to help Data Engineers streamline and automate data collection, processing, and reporting.
- Translate complex data into clear insights and actionable recommendations for technical and non-technical audiences.
- Query and manage databases using SQL and Snowflake.
- Refine reporting systems and workflows as business needs evolve.

## Your Superpowers

- Three or more years of experience in business intelligence, data analysis, or reporting.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Finance, Economics, Analytics, Business, Math, Statistics, or a related field.
- Strong SQL and Python skills with proficiency in advanced data visualization tools; Snowflake is a plus.
- Working knowledge of data modelling concepts such as star schemas and fact versus dimension tables.
- Deep expertise in statistical analysis, data pivots, predictive modelling, and scalable data solutions.
- Strong attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy and quality.
- Self-motivated, proactive, collaborative, comfortable in a fast-paced environment, and driven by process improvement and innovation.
- Strong communication skills and ability to deliver insights to diverse cross-functional audiences.
