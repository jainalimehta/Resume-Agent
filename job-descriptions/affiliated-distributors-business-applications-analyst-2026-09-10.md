# Business Applications Analyst

**Company:** Affiliated Distributors (AD)  
**Location:** Mississauga, Ontario  
**Employment:** Full-time  
**Schedule:** Hybrid, three office days and two remote days; 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., Monday–Friday  
**Travel:** Minimal, one to three times annually

## Position Summary

The Business Applications Analyst joins AD's corporate IT team and supports technical operations for the U.S. Gypsum Supply Division (GSD), U.S. Plumbing, Heating, Cooling and Piping division (PHCP), and AD Canada Operations. The role is a subject-matter resource for operational tools connecting internal teams, distributor members, and supplier partners, focusing on LBMX, Solution Center, EDI-related processes, relational databases, SQL, and Qlik reporting.

This business-operations position requires technical fluency in relational databases, SQL, BI/reporting, and EDI transaction types and business flows such as procure to pay. EDI mapping and software development are not required. The analyst serves internal users, distributors, and suppliers and helps resolve business and reporting issues.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Serve as day-to-day contact for internal users, member distributors, and suppliers regarding LBMX questions and issues.
- Maintain trading-partner information, customer locations, and cross-reference data in LBMX and related systems.
- Support and troubleshoot Solution Center configuration and functionality.
- Validate EDI transactions and understand the transaction types and business flows they support, coordinating mapping changes with LBMX or internal technical teams.
- Connect to relational databases and write SQL to extract, validate, and analyze operational and transactional data.
- Build, maintain, and distribute Qlik and other BI reports for operational and member-facing needs.
- Investigate and resolve business-process issues with members, suppliers, internal stakeholders, staff, and users.
- Develop and maintain user guides, process specifications, and documentation for LBMX, Solution Center, and reporting workflows.
- Stay current on LBMX, EDI, reporting, and industry practices.
- Identify process and reporting improvements and collaborate with vendors and members to enhance workflows.

## Requirements

- Understand general business functions and divisional or departmental activities.
- Working relational-database and SQL knowledge sufficient for queries and reporting connections.
- Experience with Qlik or a similar BI/reporting tool, including building and maintaining reports.
- Solid understanding of EDI transaction types and supported business flows such as procure to pay; mapping/development is not required.
- Comfortable supporting business users and external customers or partners.
- Clear listening, verbal, and written communication and the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical users.
- Strong learning, problem-solving, analytical, integrity, professionalism, prioritization, deadline management, and intermediate Microsoft Office skills.

## Nice to Have

- LBMX and Solution Center configuration/support.
- PHCP, HVAC, distribution, industrial supply, ERP, wholesale, or buying-group experience.
- Enterprise AI tools for report building, data analysis, and process discovery.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in business, information systems, or related field; relevant experience may substitute.
- Two to four years in business analysis, business operations, application support, or customer support/success involving data reporting.
