# CIBC — Business Systems Analyst

- Location: Toronto, 81 Bay Street, 4th Floor
- Employment: Regular, 37.5 hours weekly
- Work arrangement: Hybrid, one to three days on-site weekly

## Role

Lead complex projects to design strategic solutions aligned to CIBC's enterprise architecture roadmap. Support end-to-end project implementation and act as a business and technology subject-matter expert. Collaborate across a diverse team to create flexible, innovative, client-centred solutions.

## How Success Is Achieved

- Gather complicated business requirements with business partners.
- Facilitate solution-design discussions, build relationships, listen, and ask appropriate questions.
- Assemble client-focused solutions meeting business requirements.
- Prepare and validate solution-design documents covering scope clarification, solution requirements, and quality assurance.
- Support end-to-end implementation as an SME.
- Guide technology and project professionals and help resolve organizational, operational, and process problems.
- Investigate complex problems, interpret data, and communicate detailed information meaningfully.
- Work through relationships, teamwork, influence, trust, and accountability.

## Requirements

- Degree in Computer Science, Business, or a related field.
- At least three years in a Business Systems Analyst role leading projects and designing solutions.
- Agile methods and application/architecture development are assets.
- Skills: analytical thinking, business requirements, business systems analysis, IT process improvement, mentoring, operational efficiency, service delivery, solution design, system applications, and technical knowledge.
