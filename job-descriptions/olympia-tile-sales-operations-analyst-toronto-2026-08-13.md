# Olympia Tile — Sales Operations Analyst

- Location: Toronto, Ontario; full-time on-site
- Travel: Approximately 15 percent
- Reporting line: VP of Sales
- Role status: Newly created
- Date captured: August 13, 2026

## Company

Olympia Tile is a ceramic-tile and stone distributor founded in 1927, with Canadian and United States branches serving residential, commercial, retail, and architectural-design markets. The company emphasizes product quality, customer service, operational excellence, collaboration, innovation, and long-term development.

## Opportunity

The Sales Operations Analyst works with the VP of Sales and Regional Sales Management on strategic projects. The role connects data insight with sales execution, operational improvement, executive decision-making, and leadership reporting.

## Responsibilities

- Partner with the VP of Sales on strategic initiatives, executive reporting, business planning, growth projects, and special assignments.
- Manage and improve the internal CRM, including adoption, dashboards, customized workflows, and data rationalization.
- Evaluate sales processes and improve CRM data quality through audits and standardization.
- Analyze performance trends, margins, and regional activity and maintain forecasting models for revenue and target evaluation.
- Develop recurring KPIs and executive dashboards and translate complex data into recommendations for territories, pricing, and resource allocation.
- Support Regional Sales Managers with territory analysis and standardized productivity tools and templates.
- Partner with Marketing, Finance, Procurement, and IT to align systems, reporting, and strategic initiatives.
- Research industry and competitor developments and lead cross-functional sales-technology and operational-excellence initiatives.

## Qualifications

- Recent post-secondary degree in Business Administration, Commerce, Economics, Data Analytics, or a related field.
- One to three years in analytics, sales support, or corporate operations.
- Advanced spreadsheet and data-analysis ability, analytical curiosity, and problem-solving.
- Technical aptitude to navigate and optimize CRM databases and workflows.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills and ability to influence stakeholders at all levels.
- Microsoft Office and Google Workspace proficiency for reports, charts, presentations, and communication.
- Ability to work full-time on-site in Toronto and travel approximately fifteen percent.
- Job-poster requirements: bachelor's degree, Canadian work authorization, and at least one year of database work experience.
