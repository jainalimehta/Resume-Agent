# Data Consultant, Audit Technologies Group — KPMG in Canada

## Overview

At KPMG in Canada, our people bring their unique perspectives to Canada’s most important challenges. Here, you can build momentum that reaches beyond our business, develop skills for the future, and take ownership of your career with support at every stage. Join a firm where your career can make a difference.

The Audit Technologies Group is a part of the national audit center, audit support team. The Audit Technologies Group is tasked with implementing new technologies in the audit practice and providing digital and data & analytics services to our clients. The group works as part of a multi-disciplinary team providing and supporting KPMG proprietary and third-party data & analytics solutions to our teams.

Data consultants will work as part of the CDST to assist client-facing teams with data extraction, transformation, and load capabilities into various audit tools. In addition, they provide critical support with data analysis, reporting and data visualization. This work will enable KPMG to deliver innovative solutions to clients.

This position requires written and oral fluency in English. The successful candidate may be required to support or collaborate with English-speaking colleagues or stakeholders while at KPMG. The successful candidate may be required to create, interpret and/or apply policies, practices, laws and/or regulations during the regular course of employment.

## What You Will Do

- Work with audit engagement teams to support the use of Data and Analytics in external audits, including data acquisition, movement and transformation; use technologies and capabilities such as Alteryx, SQL, Databricks, Power BI and Microsoft PowerApps and Power Automate.
- Communicate the technical implications of D&A projects to audit engagement teams; identify issues and challenges, and provide recommendations, opportunities, and mitigation strategies.
- Work with audit engagement teams to scope D&A projects, review analytic results and deliver innovative solutions that enhance audit planning and execution.
- Collaborate with client technology teams to understand ERP environments and data architecture, assess the technical implications of data acquisition and analysis, and deliver clear, well-structured audit documentation across the D&A lifecycle, while building long-term client relationships through high-quality engagement delivery and professional networking.
- Participate in business development by identifying new opportunities and assisting with proposal development for a high-quality technology- and data-enabled audit.

## What You Bring to the Role

- Bachelor's, Diploma, or Master's degree in Computer Science, Database Management, Data Programming, Information Systems, Data Engineering, Data Analytics, Applied Computing or another IT-related field.
- Coursework or experience in Accounting and/or Finance is ideal.
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
- Strong database proficiency, such as SQL, MySQL, or Oracle, and knowledge of SQL scripting, including creation/implementation of APIs and debugging ability.
- Knowledge of data analytics, visualization, scripting, and automation tools including Alteryx, Power BI, Azure Databricks, Python, Microsoft VBA, Power Query, PowerApps, and Power Automate.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to work with multiple stakeholders, communicate priorities, and manage expectations.
- Proven time-management and organizational skills.
- Comfort managing diverse responsibilities, from administrative and operational work to project- and deadline-driven tasks.
- Resourceful and creative problem-solving skills, with a proactive approach to new ideas and ways of working.

## Keys to Success

- Clearly articulate, identify and define problems and develop creative and practical solutions for different and unique client requirements.
- Employ sound professional judgment and professional skepticism.
- Be a flexible and adaptable team player with leadership experience.
- Deliver high-quality, detail-oriented work and demonstrate sound organization and project-management skills.
