## Role Summary

Members of the Applied Managed Services and Cloud Managed Services team help organizations continuously evolve and optimize business systems, processes, and operations. The work combines technology, transformation, industry expertise, advisory, assets, analytics, and specialized skills to provide ongoing client outcomes and business value.

## Teams

- **Customer & Marketing:** sustain and grow clients' digital solutions.
- **Core Business Operations:** optimize business operations and help clients adopt new technologies.
- **Enterprise Technology & Performance:** provide long-term application support, optimization, and transformation for ERP and custom-development solutions.
- **Human Capital:** sustain and grow HR technology solutions.
- **Analytics:** operate, optimize, and transform clients' data foundations and integration activities.

## Candidate Requirements

- Completed or completing a post-secondary degree or diploma by January 2027, preferably in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Business/Commerce, Economics, Finance, Political Science, or another relevant field.
- Ability to work across industries, performing research, data analysis, and modelling to solve complex business problems.
- Comfort with ambiguity, competing deadlines, and rapidly evolving client needs.
- Effective written and verbal communication with the creativity to conceptualize and articulate new approaches and solutions.
- Curiosity, thoughtful questioning, and ability to connect ideas.
- Analytical thinking and attention to detail.
- Collaborative teamwork focused on client outcomes.
- Adaptability and ability to pivot with evolving demands.
- Leadership through initiative, anticipation, ownership, and delivery.
- Organized written and verbal communication.
