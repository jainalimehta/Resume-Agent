# AI Research Contributor

**Company:** Mercor  
**Work arrangement:** Fully remote; independent contractor  
**Commitment:** Approximately 10--20 hours per week; immediate start preferred

## Role Overview

Mercor is looking for detail-oriented individuals with two to three years of experience in STEM or non-STEM fields, or currently enrolled postgraduate students, to support a research project with a leading AI lab. Contributors will help benchmark and improve advanced AI models.

## Qualifications

- Required: access to a MacBook with an M-series chip for project tasks.
- Currently pursuing or recently completed master's studies, or two to three years of relevant experience plus a bachelor's degree from a prestigious institution.
- Strong online research and communication skills.
- Ability to gather and clearly summarize information from diverse sources.
- Excellent written communication skills.
- Interdisciplinary degree is an asset.
- Eligible devices include Apple Silicon ARM Macs, M-series MacBook Pro, or MacBook Air models running macOS 15 or higher.

## Responsibilities and Job Details

- Create high-quality research questions, answers, and evaluation materials to train advanced language models.
- Support the benchmarking and improvement of cutting-edge AI models.
- Work approximately 10--20 hours per week in a clearly structured, lower-pressure project setting.
- Work remotely and independently on a flexible schedule.

## Application and Onboarding

- Submit a resume.
- Complete a 15-minute conversation with an AI interviewer assessing research and reasoning skills.
- Complete a brief paid assessment.
- Receive follow-up within a few days regarding status and next steps.

## Contract and Payment Terms

- Independent-contractor engagement.
- Projects may be extended, shortened, or concluded early depending on project needs and performance.
- Work will not involve confidential or proprietary information belonging to another employer, client, or institution.
- Weekly payments through Stripe or Wise based on services rendered.
- Mercor cannot support H1-B or STEM OPT candidates for this opportunity.
- Qualified applicants are considered without regard to legally protected characteristics, and reasonable accommodations are available on request.
