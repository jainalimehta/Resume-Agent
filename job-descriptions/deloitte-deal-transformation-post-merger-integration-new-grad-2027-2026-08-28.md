## Role Summary

Deloitte's Deal Transformation & Post Merger Integration team helps clients solve complex business and performance challenges in business transformations, turnarounds, and M&A situations. Team members work across technology, operations, strategy, finance, and other functions.

## Responsibilities

- Support client engagement teams and key decision makers in developing and executing turnaround and M&A strategies.
- Conduct detailed quantitative and qualitative research and analysis of client, industry, and market performance; synthesize findings into practical strategies and action plans.
- Assist with assessing and configuring target operating models and identifying synergies and key value drivers.
- Identify, validate, and track synergy and value-creation opportunities to help clients achieve transaction objectives and measurable outcomes.
- Develop analyses, reports, workshops, presentations, and actionable recommendations supporting business decisions.
- Collaborate across teams to help organizations imagine, deliver, and operate their future.
- Support mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, restructurings, operating-model redesigns, and large-scale change initiatives.

## Team and Service Context

The team sits within Strategy, Risk and Transactions, Mergers & Acquisitions. Practitioners work through the M&A lifecycle, including strategy, operational readiness, due diligence, pre- and post-close planning and execution, and post-transaction transformation. Services include vision and strategy definition, target operating models, system-architecture assessment, process design, business-case development, value-creation and deal-structure assessment, operational and commercial due diligence, integration/separation planning, enterprise profitability, and business-model optimization.
