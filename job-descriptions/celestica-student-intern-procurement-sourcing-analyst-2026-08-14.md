## Opportunity

The Procurement Sourcing Analyst internship sits in Corporate Supply Chain and develops commodity and supply-chain business practices, communications, analytics, Microsoft-product proficiency, project-management experience, and interaction with global sites.

- Analytical and interpersonal ability.
- Database software and major desktop applications.
- Time management, tight-deadline performance, autonomous work after training, and teamwork.

## Activities

- Maintain or research suppliers at the commodity level.
- Research commodity-pricing trends.
- Create presentations and reports with Excel, Access, and PowerPoint.
- Run total cost of ownership analysis.
- Send and negotiate supplier quote tenders to establish cost and pricing.
- Gain exposure to SAP and internal databases and applications; potentially work with Business and IT on application maintenance and development.
- Consolidate, tabulate, analyze, and report commitments, topics, and performance measures.
- Support supply-chain buying teams during shortages or supplier-performance issues, including escalation and alternative-source recommendations.
- Support customers with product quality, serviceability, and performance issues.
- Develop and maintain business processes.
- Work autonomously on increasingly complex duties and collaborate with supervisors, colleagues, team members, and global suppliers.
