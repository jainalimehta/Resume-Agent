# TD Securities — Project Analyst

- Location: Toronto, Ontario, Canada
- Hours: 37.5
- Line of Business: Enterprise Enabling Functions
- Group: VCP, TD Securities
- Reports to: Director / Senior Project Manager
- Pay: CAD $81,600–$115,200

## Role

Conduct research, analysis, and reporting on operational and service issues. Execute smaller projects and strategic special initiatives, potentially partnering across TD Securities and enterprise functions. Support complex project/program analysis and reporting.

## Accountabilities

- Coordinate and facilitate appropriate partners and expertise for key initiatives.
- Communicate project status and escalate issues to senior management.
- Support cross-functional interdepartmental initiatives delivering value to internal or partner/customer groups.
- Provide upward feedback and analysis; execute consolidated or aggregated reporting.
- Contribute to management presentations and communications.
- Maintain risk-management and control culture aligned with enterprise frameworks, including AML and business continuity.
- Support change-management projects and document new processes.
- Communicate project status, issues, and relevant day-to-day information.
- Deliver work packages within larger programs; potentially lead low-risk Tier 5 projects.
- Develop understanding of technology/business issues and user interaction with technology.
- Potentially provide training and guidance on processes and business-as-usual activities.

## Requirements

- Build relationships with business, stakeholder, and technology partners.
- Manage workload and capacity against project milestones.
- Support collaboration, diversity, service, quality, innovation, and teamwork.
- Understand technology/business interaction through project delivery.
- Support communications and change management across stakeholders.
- Knowledge of risk management, standards, regulations, project/program planning, and implementation.
- Sound judgment, written and oral communication, collaboration, analytical tools, data methods, reporting, Microsoft Office, and Excel.
- Analyze, organize, and prioritize against multiple deadlines.
- Work independently and collaboratively; handle confidential information discreetly.
- Nice to have: systems interfaces, payment/funding processes, compensation rules, and regulatory issues.
- Experience: 3–5 years related experience.
