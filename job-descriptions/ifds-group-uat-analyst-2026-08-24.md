# UAT Analyst

**Company:** IFDS Group / International Financial Data Services (IFDS Canada)  
**Location:** 30 Adelaide Street East, Toronto, Ontario, M5C 3G9, Canada  
**Employment:** Permanent; Department: Ops Shared Services (830)  
**Salary:** CAD $58,000--$73,000

## Who we are

International Financial Data Services (IFDS) is a provider of outsourcing and technology solutions to the financial-services industry. IFDS serves global asset managers, wealth managers, banks, and insurance companies. IFDS Canada is a joint venture between State Street Corporation and SS&C Technologies.

## Role Overview

Responsible for user testing of the business-application aspects of enhancements to iFast Base, Desktop, Web, and related applications. Ensures system changes conform to business requirements and processes and to internal and external client expectations.

## Role Responsibilities

- Creates UAT test plans, test cases, and scenarios that capture client requirements and potential risk items and accurately define expected and actual results.
- Attends business and technical project meetings; acquires and clarifies requirements, concerns, and approaches from involved teams and applies that understanding to the UAT testing strategy.
- Works with internal teams to resolve environment and project issues.
- Recognizes improvement opportunities and works with leads and management toward implementation.
- Reviews business-specification documents, conducts research, and gathers project information.
- Ensures adequate testing coverage of business and functional requirements documented in release notes and project artifacts.
- Understands and tests business-improvement process or setup changes across Transfer Agency applications, including iFast Base, Desktop, and Web.
- Identifies and reports clearly documented testing defects.
- Monitors assigned-project issues and escalates and follows up as appropriate.
- Promptly identifies risk items and acts to prevent impact through testing and timely communication.
- Learns and retains Transfer Agency business processes and system setups for effective risk-based negative testing.
- Prepares PowerPoint presentations and conducts functionality walkthroughs for a Business Operations audience.
- Supports ad hoc testing requests.
- Updates UAT reporting spreadsheets for accurate statistics and metrics reporting.
