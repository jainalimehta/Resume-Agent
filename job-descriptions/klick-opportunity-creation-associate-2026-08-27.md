## Company Description

**The Klick Group**—comprising Klick Health (including Oxford PharmaGenesis, Klick Katalyst, and btwelve), Klick Media, Klick Applied Sciences (including Klick Labs), and Klick Consulting—is an ecosystem dedicated help life sciences brands achieve their full potential through best-in-class marketing and advertising, media, medical affairs and communications, market access, real-world evidence, and technology and analytics consulting — powered by 500+ in-house medical experts and award-winning data science and AI capabilities. Founded in 1997, we have offices in Toronto, New York, Oxford, London, Cambridge, Cardiff, Melbourne, Philadelphia, Saratoga Springs, São Paulo, and Singapore. One of the most-awarded health agencies on the planet, Klick is consistently recognized for its people-first culture as a Great Place to Work, Best Workplace for Women, Best Workplace for Inclusion, Most Admired Corporate Culture, and FORTUNE Best Workplace in Advertising. Klick also recently made history as Cannes Lions Healthcare Agency of the Year, Healthcare Network of the Year, and Independent Agency of the Year – Health.

**About Our Growth Team**

Our Growth team helps shape Klick’s future by identifying opportunities, driving pitch excellence, and supporting strategic pursuits. We bring clarity, coordination, and collaboration to complex new business efforts—enabling teams to do their best work and helping Klick show up at its strongest.

## Job Description

**What You'll Do**

- Conduct research on target companies, industry trends, and emerging opportunities to identify viable leads.
- Maintain and update prospecting databases, ensuring data accuracy and timeliness across tools and systems.
- Partner with internal teams to support preparation for outreach, meetings, and pitches—compiling backgrounders and summarizing client activity.
- Monitor inbound leads and outbound activities, triaging requests and ensuring timely handoff to relevant stakeholders.
- Coordinate with marketing, communications, and client service teams to ensure prospecting materials are current, relevant, and aligned with Klick's positioning.

## Qualifications

**Required**

- 1-2 years of experience in business development, sales operations, market research, or a client services role, ideally within healthcare marketing, pharma, or life sciences.
- Strong research and analytical skills, with ability to source, synthesize, and present competitive intelligence and prospect insights.
- Proficiency with CRM tools (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) and MS Office or Google Suite; experience with business intelligence tools is a plus.
- Highly organized with strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities in a deadline-driven environment.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with confidence preparing client-facing materials and briefing senior growth leaders.

**Desired**

- Interest in business development, sales, or growth strategy within a healthcare or pharmaceutical environment.
- Familiarity with Salesforce ecosystem including Marketing Cloud, Health Cloud, or custom Lightning development.
- Understanding of pharmaceutical agency capabilities, market positioning, and client decision-making processes.
- Interest in responsible AI governance, emerging foundation models, and their practical applications in healthcare.
- Comfort working across multiple teams and stakeholders in a collaborative, fast-moving environment.
- Comfort and enthusiasm for using AI as a regular part of how work gets done.
