## About the Role

SOCAN is seeking a Data Analyst for its Analytics department. The analyst will turn raw data into actionable business insights through reports and dashboards, driving improvement in key organizational initiatives. The role works closely with data engineers, BI analysts, other data analysts, and business leaders. The ideal candidate has a solid grasp of data fundamentals and is eager, curious, and committed to continuous learning, particularly with modern cloud data stacks.

English is required because the position interacts regularly with English-speaking stakeholders outside Quebec.

## Key Responsibilities

- Work directly with data engineers, BI analysts, other data analysts, and business leaders to document reporting requirements and understand business processes and their effect on data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis to clean, transform, and analyze large datasets within Databricks to identify trends, anomalies, and improvement opportunities.
- Support data-quality assurance by creating data-quality tests and plans for reporting and Lakehouse projects, helping troubleshoot discrepancies and pipeline issues.
- Work with department leaders to define and document KPIs.
- Perform root-cause analysis on KPI fluctuations and provide actionable insights.
- Collaborate on continuous development of interactive Databricks AI/BI dashboards and reports, designing and maintaining clear dashboards for stakeholders.
- Curate and document semantic layers to power natural-language querying through Genie for business users.
- Participate in daily standups, project planning sessions, and retrospectives.
- Manage, prioritize, and respond to ad-hoc data-analysis requests.

## Required Qualifications and Experience

- Strong analytical and quantitative skills, with the ability to work on large datasets while keeping the broader business picture in mind.
- Strong SQL skills and intermediate Python.
- Understanding of Spark and distributed-compute environments.
- Two or more years of experience performing data analysis with Databricks or similar technologies.
- Strong communication and ability to solve loosely defined problems with a solution-oriented mindset.
- Business writing and technical-documentation skills for technical and non-technical audiences.
- Degree in a quantitative field such as Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, or Business Analytics, or equivalent experience.
