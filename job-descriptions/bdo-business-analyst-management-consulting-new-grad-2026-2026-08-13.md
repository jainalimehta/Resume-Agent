## Opportunity

BDO's Digital Strategy & Transformation practice helps Canadian organizations solve complex business challenges and achieve practical digital outcomes involving business platforms, data, AI, strategies, roadmaps, future-ready operating models, organizational change, planning, scoping, and implementation.

The firm seeks curious, business-first, technology-aware new graduates who enjoy solving problems and engaging with clients and business and technology leaders.

## Responsibilities

- Support digital strategy engagements through research, client-data analysis, and prioritization of business needs.
- Perform data collection, market research, and business-process analysis to support creative solutions and transformation roadmaps.
- Draft client presentations, reports, and proposals; translate complex data into clear executive-level narratives.
- Participate in client interviews, discovery workshops, and design sessions while documenting insights and requirements.
- Bring forward ideas, research emerging trends such as Agentic AI, and support new offerings, intellectual property, and best practices.
- Remain current on technology and industry developments and apply those perspectives to client problems.

## Success profile

- Demonstrate Integrity, Respect, and Collaboration.
- Understand client industries, challenges, and opportunities and deliver high-quality work.
- Recommend effective service delivery and actively adopt digital tools and strategies.
- Learn continuously and grow professional expertise.

## Must-have qualifications

- Recent or upcoming university degree in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- Internship, co-op, or academic-project experience in consulting, technology advisory, strategy, or business analysis.
- Strong Microsoft Office proficiency; PowerPoint and Excel are essential.
- Foundational understanding of digital strategy and business applications of AI.
- Demonstrated issue diagnosis, logical solution design, and critical thinking.

- Structured, analytical problem-solving.
