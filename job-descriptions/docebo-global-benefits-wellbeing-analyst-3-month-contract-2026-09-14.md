## The Day-to-Day

- Power the Basics: Assist with the routine administration of US and Canadian benefits like health, dental, vision, 401(k), and RRSP, supporting seamless onboarding and open enrollment logistics.
- Navigate Leaves with Care: Learn and support leave of absence administration (e.g., US FMLA/STD, Canadian provincial leaves) and workplace accommodations by meticulously logging files, tracking timelines, and coordinating essential documentation.
- Be the First Point of Care: Act as the warm, empathetic frontline contact for routine employee benefit questions, providing stellar care or escalating complex queries to senior team members.
- Master the Details: Cross-check monthly carrier billing statements against our HRIS (Dayforce) data to ensure pinpoint accuracy in enrollment numbers and payroll deductions.
- Leverage the Future: Embrace an AI-first mindset by utilizing modern tools to streamline data entry, draft crystal-clear employee communications, and supercharge your daily task management.
- Champion Global Wellness: Support our Senior Specialist with international data projects while actively organizing and drafting communications for engaging, company-wide health and wellbeing initiatives.

- Eagle-Eyed Accuracy: You are meticulous and detail-oriented, taking immense pride in your work by double-checking data and catching those tiny, elusive errors before they become issues.

- Empathetic & Adaptable: You are customer-obsessed and highly organized, delivering warm, clear communications to employees while adapting seamlessly to shifting priorities in our fast-paced environment.
