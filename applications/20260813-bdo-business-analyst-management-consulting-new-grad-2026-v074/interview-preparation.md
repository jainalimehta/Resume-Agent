## Case interview structure

When given an unfamiliar business problem:

1. Clarify the objective, decision, stakeholders, scope, timeframe, and definition of success.
2. Restate the problem and identify the current process or operating model.
3. Break the problem into mutually clear drivers: people, process, technology, data, governance, cost, risk, and customer impact as relevant.
4. Form initial hypotheses and ask for the evidence needed to test them.
5. Analyze the facts, distinguish findings from assumptions, and identify root causes.
6. Develop options and compare value, feasibility, risk, dependencies, and change impact.
7. Recommend a practical path, immediate next steps, measures, and a phased roadmap.
8. Summarize the answer in a headline, two or three supporting points, key risk, and next action.

## Likely questions

- Why consulting? Emphasize variety of business problems, structured learning, client impact, and translating analysis into action.
- Why BDO? Connect the people-first culture, integrity/respect/collaboration, Canadian clients, practical digital outcomes, and formal growth environment.
- How do you handle ambiguity? Clarify the decision, structure the problem, state assumptions, prioritize evidence, share early progress, and iterate.
- How do you explain complex data simply? Start with the decision and headline, use only the evidence needed, define terms, visualize selectively, state limitations, and end with action.
- How do you prioritize? Compare urgency, client impact, risk, dependencies, effort, and deadlines; confirm trade-offs and communicate early.
- Tell me about AI in business. Discuss an appropriate problem, human accountability, reliable data, a measurable pilot, controls, and adoption—not novelty alone.
