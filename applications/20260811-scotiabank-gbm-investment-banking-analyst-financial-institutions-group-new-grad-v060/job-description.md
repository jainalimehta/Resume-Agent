## Who We Are

Global Banking & Markets provides investment banking, credit, risk-management, financing, mergers and acquisitions, corporate banking, institutional equity sales and trading, research, fixed income, derivatives, energy, foreign exchange, and precious-metals products and services. Analysts execute a broad range of transactions in a challenging, high-responsibility environment.

Early-career Analysts focus on client presentations, financial modelling, and research before taking increasing responsibility on live transactions under senior guidance. Analysts interact with senior team members, clients, Corporate Banking, Equity Capital Markets, Global Fixed Income, and Global Equity Derivatives. New Analysts may participate in the Capital Ideas Program for technical and professional training.

## Responsibilities

- Evaluate and analyze corporate clients' financial needs, including developing financial models.
- Create marketing materials and presentations.
- Support transaction structuring and execution with increasing responsibility.
- Identify and develop business opportunities with clients.
- Perform comprehensive financial-statement analysis and create and maintain models.
- Collect, consolidate, and interpret company and industry data.
- Perform financial and written analysis of companies and industries.
- Prepare client presentations by compiling data and assembling materials.
- Support drafting prospectuses, transaction documentation, and marketing materials.
- Apply product and capital-markets knowledge to help clients achieve financial objectives.

- Investment Banking, M&A, or private-equity experience preferred; equity research, accounting-firm corporate finance, or management consulting may be considered.
- Outstanding academic and extracurricular achievement.
- Clearly defined interest in Investment Banking and M&A.
- Strong quantitative skills focused on financial analysis, accounting, and financial theory.
- Strong written and verbal communication.
- High attention to detail.
- Ability to manage multiple simultaneous projects while maintaining high standards.
- Ability to excel under pressure and demanding deadlines.
- Team effectiveness, integrity, adaptability, energy, independent thinking, decision-making, and desire to learn.
- Related industry experience is an asset.
