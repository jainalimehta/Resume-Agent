# Workplace Operations Coordinator, SBN

Requisition ID: 270003

Location: Toronto, Ontario, Canada

## Purpose

As part of the Operations team, the incumbent supports the key enablement functions that keep Business Lines running optimally. The role provides front-line support to multiple teams by delivering exceptional service to clients and employees at the Scotiabank Bay Adelaide Centre. It requires service delivery, communication, administration, independence, organization, time management, sound judgement, knowledge of bank-related computer systems, and compliance with governing regulations, internal policies, and procedures.

## Accountabilities

### Client Experience

- Champion a customer-focused culture.
- Receive and welcome visitors and act as the central point of contact upon arrival.
- Understand the workspace, technology, and Business Lines to answer questions and support clients.
- Manage and review the Visitor Management System and prepare for appointments and bookings.
- Answer the telephone professionally, promptly, and courteously.
- Maintain a clean desk and reception area.

### Administrative Support

- Support Senior Leadership with meeting-room requests, room set-up, conference technology, food services, and reception-area maintenance.
- Accept deliveries, arrange courier pickups, and administer inbound RightFax messages.
- Support key-holder responsibilities, badge distribution, floor opening and closing, and security credentials.
- Escalate suspicious activity to security.
- Provide general administrative support and assist with ad hoc projects.
- Manage and maintain a shared inbox as the primary support channel.
- Troubleshoot standard office equipment and meeting-room technology and coordinate service calls and vendors.

### Other

- Provide contingent coverage for other reception areas and Operations Coordinators and Leads.
- Complete other relevant administrative or project duties.
- Consider the Bank's risk appetite and risk culture in day-to-day decisions.
- Pursue effective and efficient operations in accordance with Scotiabank's Values, Code of Conduct, Global Sales Principles, and business controls relating to operational, compliance, AML/ATF/sanctions, and conduct risk.
- Contribute to an inclusive, high-performance environment.

## Dimensions and Working Conditions

- Downtown Toronto at Bay and Adelaide, serving a building of approximately 2,000 employees.
- Independently manage issues raised by clients, employees, and visitors with minimal supervision.
- Remain flexible and adaptable while managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Work 37.5 hours weekly within rotating core schedules between 7:30 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.; flexible or extended hours may be required.

## Requirements

- Minimum two years of related customer-service or hospitality experience, or equivalent experience.
- Excellent and proven customer-service focus.
- Excellent verbal and written communication.
- Excellent organization, prioritization, and coordination of multiple tasks.
- Ability to work with minimum supervision and collaborate with a large team.
- Strong interpersonal skills with internal partners, external clients, and employees at all levels.
- Intermediate Microsoft Word, Excel, Outlook, OneNote, and Teams.
- Basic OfficeSpace Facility Management Tool skills.
- Knowledge of conference and meeting-room technology and video conferencing, or the ability to learn.
- Responsiveness to changing direction, priorities, and schedules.

Candidates must apply directly online. Scotiabank is committed to inclusion and accessibility throughout recruitment.
