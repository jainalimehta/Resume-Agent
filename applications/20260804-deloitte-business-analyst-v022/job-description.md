About The Team

We're growing our Product Engineering & Development team and seeking an experienced Junior Developer who can contribute to both delivery and pre-sales activities. Depending on your background, you'll work in one of several technical domains: custom development, integration/middleware, Curam (social program management), DevOps/infrastructure, AI-enhanced development, or Azure cloud engineering. Regardless of your specialty, you'll support technical solutioning with clients and help scope engagements alongside senior team members. The ideal candidate is a hands-on technologist who can clearly communicate technical concepts and contribute to the sales process when needed.

What will your typical day look like?

Development delivery and pre-sales tasks (all tracks):

Assist with the analysis, design, and implementation of AI-driven application development and Cloud-based solutions for clients.
Participate in client workshops, meetings, and knowledge-sharing sessions and collaborate with technical and business teams to document requirements and solution designs.
Contribute to the development, integration, testing, and deployment of full stack custom and extended package software applications.
Participate in application migration and modernization initiatives, including assessment, planning, and execution.
Research and experimentation with emerging application development, AI/Agentic and Cloud trends, tools, and best practices to support client engagements.
Prepare client deliverables such as presentations, reports, and technical documentation.

Depending on your technical domain, you will also:

Custom development: Develop and test application features using technologies such as Java, .NET, Python, or Node.js while following software development best practices
Integration/middleware: Support the development of APIs and integrations between applications and enterprise systems using modern integration tools and platforms
Curam: Assist with the configuration, testing, and support of Merative Curam solutions for health and human services programs
DevOps/platform engineering: Support CI/CD pipelines, cloud deployments, and infrastructure automation activities while gaining experience with DevOps tools and practices
AI-enabled delivery: Assist in building and testing AI-enabled solutions, including prompt engineering, AI integrations, and AI-assisted software development tools.
Azure cloud engineering: Support the development, deployment, and maintenance of applications and services within the Azure cloud environment.
