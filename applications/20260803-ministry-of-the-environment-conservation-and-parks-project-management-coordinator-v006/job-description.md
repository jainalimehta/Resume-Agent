Join the Environmental Monitoring Branch and play a pivotal role in driving strategic project delivery and operational excellence.

In this role, you will lead strategic, branch-wide projects that support environmental monitoring programs and modernization initiatives. Working closely with program experts and Information Technology partners, you will help ensure business needs are understood and reflected in new processes and information systems.

You will act as a bridge, helping translate operational requirements into effective solutions, ensuring priorities are aligned, projects are well managed and decision makers have the information they need.

## About the Job

In this role, you will:

- Lead complex, cross-divisional projects that support branch and ministry priorities.
- Work with environmental monitoring staff and subject-matter experts to identify business needs and opportunities for improvement.
- Represent program perspectives in modernization initiatives.
- Collaborate with IT partners to help ensure technology solutions meet business and operational needs.
- Monitor project scope, timelines, and risks and provide recommendations to support successful delivery.
- Prepare business cases, briefing materials, and other presentations for decision-makers.
- Develop strong working relationships, facilitate collaboration, resolve issues, and build consensus among stakeholders.

## Project Leadership and Delivery

You have:

- Experience leading complex projects or business-transformation initiatives from planning to implementation.
- Project leadership skills to define scope, establish priorities, manage risks, and monitor progress.
- Leadership ability to direct and coordinate the work of project staff and external consultants.
- Experience working with a range of partners, including technology teams, to support initiatives involving information-system or business-process improvements.
- The ability to balance business needs, operational requirements, and project objectives to achieve results.

## Analytical and Strategic Problem-Solving

You have:

- Strong analytical skills to assess business needs and identify practical solutions, including solutions involving digital tools or technology systems.
- Experience evaluating new processes, technologies, or service-delivery approaches that support priorities.
- The ability to assess risk, develop mitigation strategies, and resolve issues.
- The ability to provide strategic advice and recommendations supporting informed decision-making.

## Stakeholder Engagement and Communication

You have:

- Strong communication, facilitation, and relationship-management skills.
- Experience building productive relationships and working collaboratively across teams.
- The ability to explain complex concepts to technical and non-technical audiences.
- Skills in presenting, persuading, negotiating, and building consensus to navigate competing interests.
- Experience preparing business cases, decision notes, and presentations.
- Experience collaborating with technical teams to support development and implementation of business solutions.

## Knowledge

You have:

- Knowledge of project-management principles, methodologies, and best practices.
- Knowledge of business-process improvement, program delivery, and organizational-change concepts.
- Knowledge of information-management and digital-delivery concepts.
- An understanding of program and technology development processes supporting business and operational outcomes.
- Awareness of environmental-science principles, practices, and relevant provincial and federal legislation, including the Ontario Water Resources Act, Environmental Protection Act, and Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act.
