## Role Overview

We are looking for a highly organized, proactive Business Operations Coordinator to support day-to-day operations and help drive execution across multiple projects. This role is ideal for someone early in their career who demonstrates strong structure, follow-through, and maturity beyond their years.

You will work closely with internal teams and external partners, ensuring tasks move forward, deadlines are met, and accountability is maintained across both local and remote contributors.

This is an in-office role requiring strong communication, ownership, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-moving environment.

## Key Responsibilities

- Coordinate and track multiple operational projects simultaneously.
- Maintain task lists, timelines, and follow-ups to ensure execution.
- Communicate clearly with internal team members and external stakeholders.
- Hold individuals accountable to deadlines and commitments, including local and remote contributors.
- Support process documentation, organization, and basic reporting.
- Identify gaps, bottlenecks, or delays and escalate appropriately.
- Assist with day-to-day operational tasks as needed.
- Ensure high attention to detail and accuracy across all work.
- Follow through on assignments without needing repeated reminders.

## Required Qualifications

- Post-secondary education, completed or in progress, in business, operations, or a related field.
- Some work experience, including internship, co-op, or entry-level work, demonstrating organization, structure, and follow-through.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- High computer proficiency, including Microsoft applications and the ability to learn others.
- Highly organized with the ability to manage multiple tasks at once.
- Self-starter and self-learner who takes initiative.
- Comfortable working fully in-office and collaborating daily with the team.
- High level of professionalism, reliability, and maturity.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working with task-management tools, spreadsheets, or basic project tracking.
- Exposure to working with remote team members.
- Strong academic performance demonstrating discipline and consistency.

## What Success Looks Like

- Tasks move forward without constant reminders.
- Deadlines are consistently met.
- Team members are clear on expectations and follow-ups.
- Communication is structured, concise, and thoughtful.
- Operations feel organized, predictable, and controlled.

## Why This Role Matters

This role is foundational to keeping operations running smoothly. The Business Operations Coordinator plays a key part in turning plans into execution and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.
