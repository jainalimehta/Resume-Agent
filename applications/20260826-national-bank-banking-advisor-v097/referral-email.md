Jainali has worked in client support and process coordination, including managing documentation and updates between students and institutions. She has been transparent that she has not yet worked as a Canadian Banking Advisor and does not currently hold the mutual fund licence. She is prepared to learn National Bank's products, compliance and referral procedures and pursue the licence required for progression. I believe her combination of client care, digital literacy, discretion, and learning agility could translate well to National Bank's development model.

Thank you,

[Friend Name]  
[Title / Branch, if accurate]  
[Contact details]

## Important wording boundary

Use “I know Jainali personally” unless the friend has genuinely supervised or worked with Jainali. Do not write “I have worked closely with her,” “I can vouch for her professional performance,” or any manager/staff collaboration claim unless that relationship is true and defensible.
