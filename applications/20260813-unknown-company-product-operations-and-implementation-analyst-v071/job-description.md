# Product Operations and Implementation Analyst

## About the Position

Reporting to the Manager, Product Operations and Implementation, the Product Operations and Implementation Analyst assists Product Development/Solutions and Product Operations teams with project and product planning from an implementation and program/project management perspective.

## Requirements

- 2+ years of mutual fund/investment industry experience
- University degree, or equivalent work experience
- Strong relationship building skills
- Effective oral and written communication skills
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail

## Nice to Haves

- PMP designation is an asset

## Responsibilities

- Assist the Project Implementation Team to work with Product Solutions/Development and Operations to create project plans and target timelines.
- Share and deliver the Product Roadmap and provide updates to key business partners at FIC, FFIO, GPS, FTO, Legal, State Street, and others as required.
- Develop a project plan for project-initiation deliverables, including product details and approvals, completing product intake, meeting with key business partners, and project planning.
- Help the Project Implementation Team work with PGO and I.S. Managers to plan upcoming projects and resource requirements.
- Work with business partners including Legal, Regulatory Reporting, FTO, Compliance, and FFIO to plan upcoming projects and key considerations.
- Assist with product initiatives and fund events that require project-management expertise, including ETF exchange migration, ETF name changes, and clearing-broker onboarding.
- Assist with management of schedules, deliverables, risks, and issues in workstreams managed within the Product department.
- Assist the Project Implementation Team and Product Solutions/Development and Operations to conduct gap analysis and determine net-new requirements and estimated timelines.
- Work with the Project Implementation Team on post-project review debriefs and recommendations with Product Development/Solutions and Product Operations.

## Benefits

- Base salary and discretionary performance bonus.
- RRSP contribution.
- Competitive annual range of $79,000 to $101,000 based on experience and qualifications.
- After six months of employment, employer RRSP contribution with no employee matching required.
