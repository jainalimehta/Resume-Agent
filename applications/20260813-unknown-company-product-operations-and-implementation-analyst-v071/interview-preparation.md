## Project-Initiation Framework

1. Define the objective, business case, sponsor, users, scope, exclusions, and expected value.
2. Identify stakeholders, governance, approvals, legal/regulatory dependencies, systems, data, and vendors.
3. Break deliverables into a work breakdown structure with owners, dependencies, estimates, and milestones.
4. Record assumptions, constraints, risks, issues, and decisions.
5. Establish status-reporting cadence, escalation thresholds, change control, and acceptance criteria.
6. Baseline the plan only after key owners validate feasibility and dependencies.

## Gap-Analysis Framework

- Define current state and target state.
- Compare process, product, data, system, control, regulatory, operational, and reporting dimensions.
- Classify each difference as net-new, changed, reusable, retired, or unresolved.
- Identify affected stakeholders, dependencies, risks, approvals, effort, and target timing.
- Prioritize gaps by business value, regulatory necessity, risk, and dependency.
- Convert approved gaps into traceable requirements and plan updates.

## Fund-Product Knowledge to Learn Before Interview

- Mutual fund structure: fund, manager, trustee/custodian, transfer agent, distributor/dealer, administrator, and unitholder.
- NAV, subscriptions, redemptions, distributions, fees, classes/series, and settlement basics.
- ETF creation/redemption at a high level and how an ETF differs operationally from a conventional mutual fund.
- Product lifecycle: concept, feasibility, approvals, regulatory/legal documentation, operational setup, systems/data readiness, launch, monitoring, changes, and closure.
- Fund events: launches, mergers, terminations, name changes, fee changes, exchange migrations, and service-provider changes.
- Why legal, compliance, regulatory reporting, operations, technology, finance, custodians, and transfer agents create critical dependencies.

Verify employer-specific products and current Canadian regulatory requirements from primary sources before an interview.
