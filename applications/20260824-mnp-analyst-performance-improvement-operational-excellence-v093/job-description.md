# Analyst — Consulting, Performance Improvement & Operational Excellence

**Company:** MNP  
**Location:** Ontario, Canada; client-site work and travel required  
**Opportunity:** Current  
**Salary:** CAD $50,000--$70,000

## About the job

Inspirational, innovative and entrepreneurial — this is how MNP describes its empowered teams. The Consulting — Performance Improvement & Operational Excellence team enhances enterprise value through operational and business improvement. The Analyst will solve ambiguous business challenges and learn to develop and implement solutions, improve profitability, and communicate ideas to C-suite and frontline client stakeholders.

MNP is a Canadian accounting, tax, and business-advisory firm serving the mid-market.

## Responsibilities

- Gather and analyze complex data to identify organizational challenges across operations, supply chain, finance and accounting, and human resources.
- Develop solutions to improve organizational productivity and profitability.
- Propose and assist in implementing process changes using Lean, Six Sigma, or another process-improvement methodology.
- Implement designed solutions and act as a clear change-management communicator with diverse groups.
- Develop positive client relationships at peer and management levels.
- Develop and implement key performance indicators.
- Assist in strategic planning, business planning, feasibility studies, process and performance improvement, organizational effectiveness, and analytical studies, independently and within project teams.
- Propose and create planning, execution, and reporting tools.
- Coordinate project logistics with clients and stakeholders in accordance with project plans.
- Analyze and interpret qualitative, quantitative, and financial data and present findings clearly and concisely.
- Collaborate with clients to develop a management operating system supporting operational excellence.
- Support the design, development, formatting, and proofreading of professional reports, proposals, and correspondence.
- Provide timely, accurate, and professional client service.
- Support continuing improvement in project methods and technical knowledge.
- Support MNP Consulting presentations, articles, workshops, and community activities.
- Maintain flexibility to travel as necessary.

## Skills and Experience

- Undergraduate degree in business, economics, or a related field.
- Competitive sports, music, or arts involvement is an asset.
- Lean Six Sigma certification is an asset.
- Comfortable with ambiguity and non-straightforward challenges.
- Strong critical-thinking, analytical, written-communication, and quantitative skills.
- Able to assess a situation quickly and identify improvement opportunities.
- Passionate about developing the potential of individuals and organizations.
- Motivated by tangible organizational and financial gains.
- Flexibility to work at client sites 50--75% of the time within Ontario and ability to drive to locations outside public-transit routes.
- Flexibility to travel up to 20% within Ontario or Canada.

MNP particularly values candidates who are driven to learn, humble, intentional about growing a business career, and able to learn technical gaps.
