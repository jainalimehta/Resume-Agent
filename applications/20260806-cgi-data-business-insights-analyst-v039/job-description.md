# Data & Business Insights Analyst

## Position Description

This role is hybrid and requires you to be at our downtown Toronto and/or Client office at a minimum 2 days per week subject to change at any time.

We are seeking a highly analytical and collaborative Data & Business Insights Analyst to join our team. In this role, you will transform complex data into meaningful insights that support business decision-making and operational excellence. You will work closely with stakeholders to understand business needs, develop impactful dashboards and reports, and provide data-driven recommendations that help drive organizational success.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment, enjoys solving complex business challenges, and has a passion for data visualization and storytelling.

## Your Future Duties and Responsibilities

- Independently design, develop, and deliver dashboards and reporting solutions, including Power BI dashboards.
- Analyze and interpret data from multiple sources, including platforms such as LiveLink, ServiceNow, and other enterprise systems.
- Quickly understand data structures, business processes, and stakeholder requirements to deliver actionable insights.
- Translate complex data sets into clear, concise, and executive-level summaries and presentations.
- Support business workshops and planning sessions through data-driven analysis, including capacity planning, service level agreement (SLA) analysis, resource planning and forecasting, and operational performance reporting.
- Partner with business stakeholders to identify reporting needs and recommend data-driven solutions.
- Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and integrity across reports and dashboards.
- Continuously identify opportunities to improve reporting processes, analytics capabilities, and business outcomes.

## Required Qualifications

- Experience developing dashboards and reports using Power BI.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work independently.
- Experience working with and analyzing data from multiple systems and sources.
- Ability to understand business requirements and translate them into meaningful reporting and analytics solutions.
- Strong communication and presentation skills, including the ability to explain insights to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Experience creating executive-level reporting and data-driven recommendations.
- Proficiency in data analysis, data visualization, and reporting best practices.
- Strong attention to detail and commitment to data quality.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working with platforms such as ServiceNow, LiveLink, or similar enterprise systems.
- Knowledge of capacity planning, workforce management, operational metrics, or SLA reporting.
- Familiarity with SQL, data modeling, and business intelligence methodologies.
- Experience facilitating or supporting stakeholder workshops and requirements-gathering sessions.
