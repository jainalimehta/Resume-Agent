# Business Intelligence Analyst

**Company:** Hard Rock Digital

**Team:** Financial Planning & Analysis

**Location:** Not supplied in the pasted posting

## What are we building?

Hard Rock Digital is focused on becoming the best online sportsbook, casino, and social gaming company in the world. The company is building new products and technologies for millions of consumers and cares about each customer's interaction, experience, behavior, and insight.

Rooted in the kindred spirits of Hard Rock and the Seminole Tribe of Florida, Hard Rock Digital brings the Hard Rock gaming, entertainment, and hospitality brand into the digital space.

## What's the position?

Join the Financial Planning & Analysis team as a Business Intelligence Analyst and transform data into strategic insights that drive business decisions.

- Design and maintain automated revenue dashboards and reports in Tableau tracking player behavior and wagering results across multiple states for iGaming and Sportsbook verticals.
- Conduct variance analysis on revenue trends and translate findings into actionable recommendations for leadership.
- Partner with FP&A to forecast revenue performance and identify growth opportunities or risks.
- Analyze player cohorts to identify behavioral trends and predict lifetime-value opportunities.
- Ensure data quality and integrity of revenue reporting while collaborating with Finance and Accounting teams.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Finance, Accounting, or a related field.
- Two to three years in Business Intelligence or Quantitative Analysis.
- Strong Excel skills, including pivot tables, complex formulas, and data modelling.
- Proficiency in SQL for data extraction and transformation.
- Experience building dashboards and visualizations in Tableau.
- Strong communication skills and ability to present insights to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Ability to work in a team while balancing multiple individual projects.

## Preferred

- Master's degree in Business Analytics, Statistics, or a related field.
- Sportsbook, casino, or online-gaming experience.
- Exposure to revenue-recognition principles or FP&A processes.
- Exposure to AI tools that assist or improve work streams.
