# Entry-Level Data Analyst

**Company:** Jobright

**Location:** Remote (Canada)

Jobright is your personal AI job search agent that transforms the way you do job search from solo, time-consuming efforts to a fast, expert-guided journey, simplifying every job search step and accelerating your route to the best job outcomes. The Entry-Level Data Analyst will build reliable reporting and business-intelligence systems, helping teams understand company performance through consistent metrics, dashboards, and data models.

## Why Join Us

- Build real, production AI agents used by real users
- High ownership and impact
- Work at the intersection of AI, agents, and product
- Shape how people experience AI-driven job search

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain dashboards and recurring reports
- Define consistent business metrics and reporting standards
- Write SQL queries and create reusable analytical datasets
- Automate recurring reporting workflows
- Investigate data-quality and reporting issues
- Support business planning and performance reviews

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Business Analytics, Information Systems, Economics, or a related field
- Strong SQL and spreadsheet skills
- Understanding of relational data and business metrics
- Excellent attention to detail and accuracy
- Ability to manage multiple reporting requests remotely

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with Tableau, Looker, Power BI, or similar tools
- Familiarity with data warehouses and data modeling
- Experience with Python or reporting automation
- Previous internship or business-intelligence project experience
