# Jr. Data & AI Analyst

**Company:** Redion, part of Generali Group

**Location:** Toronto, Ontario (hybrid)

**Compensation:** CAD 60,000--70,000 plus 8% bonus

**Vacancy:** One new full-time position

Redion is seeking a forward-thinking Jr. Data & AI Analyst for its Canadian IT team. Reporting to the Head of IT, with a dotted line to the Lead Data & Business Analyst, the role supports local operations by managing and optimizing data flows across a hybrid cloud environment.

## Responsibilities

- Support the automated movement and lifecycle of policy and claims data into AWS Redshift and Microsoft Fabric, using AWS Glue and Dataflow Gen2 for ETL pipelines.
- Assist with Power BI semantic models, DAX, and Power Query for reporting.
- Create and maintain interactive Power BI dashboards and advanced Excel reports; identify actionable trends, patterns, and operational insights.
- Use AWS SageMaker and Fabric AI tools to support predictive-model training and deployment.
- Track KPIs and provide data-backed recommendations to leadership.
- Partner with cross-functional teams, explain data lineage, and translate technical findings for technical and non-technical audiences.

## Qualifications

- University or college degree in Computer Science, Management Information Systems, Computer Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.
- High interest or foundational experience in AWS Redshift and Glue and Microsoft Fabric and Dataflow Gen2.
- Strong SQL, Power BI with a DAX focus, and advanced Excel including pivot tables and complex formulas.
- Python with Pandas and NumPy is highly preferred.
- Foundational understanding of insurance concepts such as underwriting, claims, and risk assessment is valued.
- Excellent written and verbal English communication and ability to map and explain complex data flows.

Redion emphasizes care, agility, international collaboration, technology-enabled insurance and assistance services, inclusion, and professional growth.
