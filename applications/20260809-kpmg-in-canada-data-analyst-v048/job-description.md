Reporting to the Tax Regional Financial Analyst, the Data Analyst will support the Financial Analyst in providing teams with required information throughout each month. This work will include the analysis of monthly results, financial planning, forecasting, and providing other complex analyses and recommendations to support strategic business decisions.

What You Will Do

Assist the Regional Financial Analyst with the preparation of the monthly financial reports
Work with Excel and PowerBI and SmartView Query reporting tools to develop various adhoc analyses and reports.
Occasional creation of Macros/VBA would be an asset.
Work with the Regional Financial Analyst to help prepare the annual budget
Provide analysis and recommendations to help the Regional financial Analyst to support strategic business decisions
Support the Firm strategy of identifying opportunities to streamline processes and continuously explore opportunities to find efficiencies and improve the services provided

At times, business needs arise, and employees are required and agree to work beyond their normal workday or work week to fulfill the accountabilities required for their job. Likewise, people need extra time to devote to personal matters, and our approach to flexibility provides for this.

What You Bring To The Role

Bachelor’s degree or an equivalent combination of education and relevant experience.
Advanced technical skills working with Excel & PowerBI. Experience with SmartView would also be a definite asset.
Ability to organize and prioritize in a fast-paced environment with tight deadlines
Self-motivated, self-starter displaying high initiative, willingness to learn, and ability to operate independently
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in order to communicate to internal and external stakeholders, and with all levels of the firm
Capability to work in a collaborative environment
MS Office – intermediate to advanced level in Excel

The following software knowledge is ideal

EPM (Enterprise Performance Management) or HFM – (Hyperion Financial Management)
BO for SAP / Hana

AI Usage

We embrace the use of artificial intelligence (AI) to enhance the candidate experience and streamline our recruitment processes.
