# Junior Business Analyst — SAP Finance

The client is seeking a Junior Business Analyst with strong SAP Finance experience to support financial systems, business process improvements, requirements analysis, testing, documentation, training, and implementation activities. The successful candidate will work closely with business users, technical teams, project stakeholders, and other cross-functional teams to analyze financial processes, identify improvement opportunities, translate business requirements into functional solutions, and support SAP-related projects from planning through post-go-live.

## Key Responsibilities

- Document and analyze business, functional, and detailed requirements.
- Review business requirements and provide recommendations to ensure alignment with business and system needs.
- Prepare and maintain project, process, functional, and system documentation.
- Develop test plans, test cases, use cases, user stories, scenarios, and testing documentation.
- Prepare and maintain SAP business process documentation using appropriate process repositories.
- Participate in project planning, change management, communications, testing, training, go-live, and post-go-live support activities.
- Prepare functional specifications for SAP development and system changes and collaborate with developers as required.
- Analyze existing financial workflows, identify inefficiencies, and recommend SAP-enabled process improvements.
- Collaborate with business users, technical teams, finance stakeholders, and cross-functional project teams.
- Work closely with end users to understand and document day-to-day operational processes.
- Support SAP end-user training and contribute to training and transition-to-support activities.

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Business, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and relevant accounting/financial management experience.
- Professional accounting designation is an asset.
- 3–5 years of experience in SAP Finance or related roles, including SAP implementation and end-user support.
- Strong SAP Business Analyst project experience.
- Hands-on experience with SAP Financial modules, particularly FI and CO.
- Understanding of SAP integration with other business systems.
- Experience with financial accounting and reporting processes.
- Experience with SAP business processes, requirements analysis, functional specifications, testing, and implementation.
- Experience preparing and maintaining project and process documentation.
- Strong understanding of financial principles and business processes.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking abilities.
- Demonstrated ability to collaborate with stakeholders and cross-functional teams.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working with SAP S/4HANA.
- Experience with SAP Fiori and related reporting tools.
- Experience in municipal accounting or public-sector financial environments.
- Experience supporting deployment of new business processes.
- Experience with end-user training and transition-to-support activities.
- Experience participating in SAP testing, including functional, integration, system, regression, performance, and user acceptance testing.
- Experience with change management and business process improvement initiatives.
