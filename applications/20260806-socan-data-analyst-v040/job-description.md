# Data Analyst — SOCAN

- Location: Toronto, Ontario or Montreal, Quebec
- Position type: Permanent full-time
- Hiring range: $71,920 to $89,900
- Work model: Hybrid
- Language: English required; French is an asset

SOCAN is a not-for-profit copyright collective that supports songwriters, composers and music publishers through licensing, global royalty collection and advocacy.

## Role

The Data Analyst will turn raw data into actionable business insights through reports and dashboards. The analyst will collaborate with data engineers, BI analysts, other data analysts and business leaders, and will contribute to organizational initiatives using a modern cloud data stack.

## Responsibilities

- Document reporting requirements and understand business processes and their effects on data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis to clean, transform and analyze large datasets in Databricks; identify trends, anomalies and improvement opportunities.
- Create data-quality tests and plans for reporting and Lakehouse projects; help troubleshoot discrepancies and pipeline issues.
- Work with department leaders to define and document KPIs.
- Perform root-cause analysis of KPI fluctuations and provide actionable insights.
- Collaborate on interactive Databricks AI/BI dashboards and reports.
- Curate and document semantic layers that support Genie natural-language querying.
- Participate in standups, project planning and retrospectives.
- Prioritize and respond to ad hoc analysis requests.

## Qualifications

- Strong quantitative and analytical skills and ability to work with large datasets while retaining the broader business context.
- Strong SQL, intermediate Python and an understanding of Spark and distributed computing.
- Two or more years performing data analysis using Databricks or similar technologies.
- Clear communication, solution-oriented problem solving, business writing and technical documentation for technical and non-technical audiences.
- Degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, Business Analytics or another quantitative field, or equivalent experience.

## Assets and competencies

- Passion for music and French fluency are assets.
- Critical thinking, problem solving, emotional intelligence, continuous learning and process orientation.
