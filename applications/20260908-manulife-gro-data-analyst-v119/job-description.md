At Manulife, we believe in investing in the future starting with you. GRO is Manulife's flagship early-talent program and provides an 18-month structured experience with learning, mentorship, leadership exposure, and work across Customer Experience, Data Analytics, and Continuous Improvement. The Data Office is a specialized stream within GRO Operations.

## Position Responsibilities

As a Data Office New Graduate, help design and deliver data, automation, and AI-enabled solutions that improve work and decision-making.

- Collaborate across teams to deliver high-impact, end-to-end solutions.
- Contribute across the full lifecycle, from idea to deployment and ongoing improvement.
- Identify opportunities to use data, automation, and AI to drive measurable outcomes.
- Communicate effectively and continuously grow technical, analytical, and business skills.
- Support client onboarding, shareholder record keeping, data management, and dashboard creation.

Depending on interests and team placement, work may include:

### Data Solutions Engineer

- Build end-to-end data solutions spanning ingestion, transformation, modelling, and visualization.
- Develop scalable data pipelines and architectures for analytics and AI use cases.
- Translate business needs into dashboards, reports, and data products such as Power BI.
- Apply data-governance and quality practices to create trusted insights.

### Business Analyst — Digitisation and Automation

- Partner with stakeholders to understand challenges and identify automation and improvement opportunities.
- Convert business needs into requirements, user stories, and process maps.
- Support analysis for automation and AI initiatives, including prioritization and benefits tracking.
- Support testing, change readiness, stakeholder communication, and adoption.

### Developer — Automation and Digital Solutions

- Design and build automation and digital solutions using Power Platform, Azure, and modern programming tools.
- Develop AI-enabled applications and workflows using Azure AI, Copilot, and related technologies.
- Convert business requirements into scalable, user-friendly technical solutions.
- Optimize, troubleshoot, and enhance applications for continuous improvement.

## Required Qualifications

- Expected to graduate in Spring 2027 from an accredited university with a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.
- Strategic thinker with a customer-centric focus.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, using data to tell a story and support decisions.
- Microsoft Power Suite knowledge or comparable automation and data-visualization tools is a strong asset.
- Working knowledge of Python for scripting, automation, integration, or data-related tasks.
- Exposure to Azure, APIs, cloud concepts, or low-code platforms is an asset.
- Curiosity, creativity, openness to new perspectives, and willingness to challenge the status quo.
- Strong work ethic with a proactive and flexible attitude.
- Excellent time management, planning, and communication skills.
- Ability to build relationships and collaborate with stakeholders.
- Understanding of financial services, including wealth and asset management.
