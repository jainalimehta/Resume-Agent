**Detailed Job Description:**

Design, develop, and maintain enterprise Power BI dashboards, reports, and data models. Collaborate with business stakeholders and technical teams to gather requirements and deliver BI solutions. Develop and optimize SQL queries and data transformations, integrate data from multiple sources, support Kyvos analytics environments, implement security and governance standards, and provide technical guidance to junior team members.

**Top 3 Required Skills:**

Power BI (DAX, Power Query, Data Modelling, RLS, Power BI Service) Advanced SQL Development & Performance Tuning, Kyvos Analytics Platform Experience

**Top 3 Preferred Skills:**

Azure Data Platform (Synapse, Data Factory, Azure SQL, Databricks) Data Warehousing & Dimensional Modelling, Agile/Scrum Methodologies and Power BI Certifications

Education Requirements: Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Systems, Analytics, or a related field.

Salary Range - CA$ 100,000 - CA$ 120,000 Per Year
