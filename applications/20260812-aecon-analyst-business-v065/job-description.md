## What You'll Do Here

### Process Mapping & Continuous Improvement Support

- Support process discovery, documentation, and mapping (current-state, future-state) for Industrial workflows and operational processes into automation-ready logic and data flows.
- Assist with digital initiatives by mapping observed issues into improvement opportunities.
- Support value stream analysis and identification of manual, repetitive, or high-risk process steps suitable for automation.

### Power Platform Development & Workflow Maintenance

- Build, enhance, and maintain Power Automate flows, including document processing, approvals, notifications, and system integrations.
- Support development, maintenance, and troubleshoot issues of Power Apps used for data intake, process tracking, and operational workflows.
- Support testing, deployment, and post-go-live stabilization of new digital configuration solutions.

### Data, Analytics & Microsoft Fabric Support

- Support data ingestion, transformation, and modeling using Microsoft Fabric (OneLake, pipelines, semantic models).
- Assist in maintaining datasets and data pipelines used for reporting, automation, and AI-driven insights.
- Support Power BI dashboards by ensuring data accuracy, refresh reliability, and alignment with process logic.

### Coding, Automation Logic & Technical Support

- Support automation logic using expressions, conditions, variables, and structured data handling within Power Platform.
- Assist with light scripting or coding tasks (e.g., SQL queries, Python for automation support, data preparation).
- Support AI-enabled workflows (AI Builder, document intelligence, classification models) by maintaining inputs, outputs, and integrations.
- Help ensure automation solutions remain scalable, secure, and aligned with enterprise standards.

### Cross-Functional Collaboration & Enablement

- Work closely with Project teams to understand workflows and data requirements.
- Support communication between technical solutions and operational users.
- Assist with training, knowledge transfer, and adoption of digital tools.
