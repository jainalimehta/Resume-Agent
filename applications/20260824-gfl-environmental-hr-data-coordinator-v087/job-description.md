**Company:** GFL Environmental

## About Us

GFL is one of the largest diversified environmental services companies in North America, providing comprehensive solid waste management services from its platform of facilities throughout Canada and 18 U.S. states. Recognized by our signature fleet of bright green trucks and equipment, we offer a wide range of environmental and industrial services to businesses, communities and households, providing a consolidated and sophisticated approach to meeting our customers’ needs. One of the keys to our success lies in the diversity of our services and our ability to deliver robust integrated solutions, all from a single efficient company. We believe that, by providing safe, accessible and cost-effective solutions, we encourage greater environmental responsibility and allow our customers and the communities we serve to be Green for Life.

## The Role

This role will serve as the front-line administrator for entering, processing and auditing sensitive HRIS data. The HR Data Coordinator is also responsible for maximizing technological capabilities to reduce manual processes, preparing ad-hoc/scheduled reporting and serving as a resource for process-improvement tasks and special projects. This position will also execute a variety of tasks associated with the on-boarding process.

### Key Responsibilities

- **Data Management & Onboarding:** Manage organization-wide data entry and tracking for new hires, job changes, and acquisitions, while maintaining comprehensive electronic personnel records for current and former employees.
- **System Auditing & Compliance:** Conduct regular audits of manager/talent team submissions, system approval workflows, and onboarding compliance to ensure maximum data accuracy and regulatory alignment.
- **Managerial Support & Guidance:** Serve as the primary HR resource and guide for managers regarding position management, system processes, and HR data inquiries.
- **Reporting & Analytics:** Facilitate data requests by assessing feasibility, generating ad-hoc reports, and ensuring data availability across the HR department.
- **Team Collaboration & Training:** Partner with internal HR Operations and broader team members to assist with orientation, compliance follow-ups, and training delivery as needed.

### What We’re Looking For

- **Education & Experience:** Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent experience) paired with 1–2 years in an administrative role supporting multiple managers.
- **Technical Proficiency:** Strong Microsoft Office skills, with a preferred background or familiarity in Workday.
- **Organization & Detail Focus:** Exceptionally detail-oriented with the organizational skills necessary to work independently and manage time wisely.
- **Adaptability & Multi-tasking:** Proven ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment while efficiently handling multiple assigned tasks.
- **Communication & Service:** Excellent verbal communication, phone etiquette, and customer service skills to effectively collaborate with others.
