# Junior Data Analyst

Company: Not identified in the supplied posting

Location: Remote, Canada

## Role

The Junior Data Analyst supports data-driven decisions across the organization. The position analyzes data, develops actionable insights, and creates Power BI dashboards and DAX reports that help stakeholders track business performance.

## Responsibilities

- Collect, clean, validate, and analyze data from multiple sources.
- Design, develop, and maintain Power BI dashboards and reports.
- Create and optimize DAX measures, calculated columns, and KPIs.
- Generate insights and recommendations from trends and business requirements.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define reporting needs and deliver analytical solutions.
- Maintain data accuracy, consistency, and reporting quality.

## Required Qualifications

- At least one year of experience in data analysis, business intelligence, or a related role.
- Hands-on Power BI dashboard development and DAX reporting.
- Strong Microsoft Excel and data-analysis skills.
- SQL knowledge for extraction and manipulation.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently in a remote environment.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Data-visualization best practices.
- ETL processes and data modelling.
- Exposure to cloud-based analytics platforms.
