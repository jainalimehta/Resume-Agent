# Operations Analyst

**Company:** Cidel

**Location:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada

*This posting is for an existing position that is currently vacant.*

## About Cidel

Cidel is a Canadian-based global private bank and asset manager. It helps high-net-worth individuals, families, and institutions protect and grow their wealth. Its integrated services include Asset Management; Planning, Structuring & Trust; and Specialized Banking. Cidel combines the scale and capability of a large global bank with the continuity and service commitment of a boutique firm.

## Job Purpose

Assist with day-to-day responsibilities in the operations team, including administration of custody relationships and ensuring custodian records are current, accurate, and allocated to the appropriate client.

## Key Areas of Accountability

- Reconcile custodian records with the internal database.
- Match daily cash balances between custodian accounts and the internal database.
- Instruct the custodian to move cash to and from bank accounts daily.
- Provide comprehensive explanations for outages.
- Allocate dividends, interest, other income, and process corporate actions.
- Resolve outages through collaboration with operations employees, trading, and custodian operations.
- Submit monthly cash-reconciliation reports to custodian auditors.
- Communicate problems with partners, relationship management, and other internal and external stakeholders.
- Respond to Client Relationship Manager questions.
- Complete monthly and year-end U.S.-dollar source-income reconciliations.
- Enter foreign-exchange account transactions and allocate dividends and income.
- Reconcile cash holdings between the custodian and internal database monthly.
- Complete daily reconciliations, identify outages, and correct them as required.
- Update pricing.
- Complete account-opening procedures and open or close custodian relationships.
- Proof monthly statements.
- Monitor, review, and balance account activity.
- Report status of custodian relationships and significant information.
- Review fees and investigate custody inquiries.
- Provide backup support to team members and the department.
- Review and update custodian and broker standard settlement instructions.
- Monitor trades and investigate and resolve unmatched or failed trades.

## Tax Responsibilities

- Prepare and audit tax packages.
- Proof data.
- Prepare and audit T3, T5, T5008, and NR4 slips.
- Enter authorized error-account transactions and collect authorization forms.
- Reconcile error-account balances monthly.
- Calculate and track foreign-exchange profits and losses after cash-management clears.
- Report total monthly profits for clearing purposes.
- Complete audit and statement requests.

## Key Competencies

- Strong problem-solving skills.
- Strong Excel skills and understanding of IT solutions.
- Attention to detail and persistence.
- Ability to communicate problems and solutions.
- General understanding of an investment-operations environment.

## Credentials and Experience

- Undergraduate degree.
- Two years of finance-industry experience.
- Canadian Securities Course preferred.
