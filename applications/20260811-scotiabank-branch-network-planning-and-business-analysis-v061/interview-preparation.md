## How to Support a Branch-Planning Project

Present this as a proposed approach, not previous branch-planning ownership.

1. Confirm the decision required, sponsor, scope, timeline, approval path, and success measures.
2. Identify contributors from regional teams, Real Estate, Optimization, Finance, operations, and risk.
3. Maintain a tracker with deliverable, owner, due date, dependency, status, issue, and next action.
4. Gather inputs using defined templates and flag missing, inconsistent, or outdated information.
5. Summarize current status, evidence, options, costs, benefits, risks, assumptions, and recommendation.
6. Record decisions, actions, owners, and deadlines; distribute follow-ups promptly.
7. Escalate material risks with evidence, impact, options, and a suggested next step.
8. Preserve version control and traceability for approved materials.

Keep one message per slide, cite sources and assumptions, and complete a final numbers, spelling, alignment, and version check. Microsoft Office is verified; advanced executive-deck ownership is not.

## Business-Case Framework

1. Define the problem, objective, scope, stakeholders, constraints, and baseline.
2. Develop credible options, including a status-quo comparison.
3. Document assumptions, one-time and recurring costs, qualitative and measurable benefits, dependencies, and risks.
4. Compare implementation effort, customer impact, operating impact, and financial implications.
5. Test sensitivity to important assumptions and identify what could change the recommendation.
6. Recommend an option with rationale, controls, milestones, owners, and success KPIs.

Study payback, ROI, NPV, and scenario analysis, but do not claim professional financial-modelling or business-case ownership.
