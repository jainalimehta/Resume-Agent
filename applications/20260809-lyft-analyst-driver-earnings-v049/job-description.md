# Analyst, Driver Earnings

Company: Lyft

Location: Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Work arrangement: Hybrid, at least three in-office days per week, including Mondays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays

Expected base pay: CAD $83,800–$104,750

## About the role

At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. The Driver Earnings team manages driver earnings products and supports business goals related to financial metrics and marketplace performance. The team improves the automation and execution of earnings products, develops new earnings products, and manages their financial performance.

The analyst will work with cross-functional stakeholders to analyze and improve existing earnings products and manage their financial outcomes within Marketplace. The role will influence strategy through data analysis, insights, and storytelling, providing recommendations on experimentation and product improvements.

## Responsibilities

- Support marketplace operations with analysis, product recommendations, and stakeholder reporting.
- Become an expert in marketplace trends and support weekly reporting structures for the Rideshare organization.
- Build domain knowledge in the earnings-lever portfolio and support cross-functional product initiatives.
- Analyze marketplace product and financial levers and provide actionable insights on improvements and problem areas.
- Evaluate decision-making frameworks critically and identify improvement and business-growth opportunities.
- Prepare regular business reviews for leadership and highlight key focus areas.
- Work with stakeholders and other teams to track, report, and reconcile budget allocation across marketplace programs.
- Analyze data, conduct research, and synthesize feedback into plans, processes, and playbooks.

## Experience

- One to three years of experience in a high-growth environment in data and/or financial analytics.
- Strong attention to detail and experience developing processes that reduce human error.
- Proficiency in SQL and quantitative analysis, including analyzing substantial data, identifying insights, dissecting business issues, and drawing actionable conclusions.
- Outcome-driven recommendations and the ability to develop a story and execution plan supporting goals and objectives.
- Exceptional listening, written, and oral communication, including audience-specific presentation of findings and recommendations.
- Drive to understand core issues and uncover solutions.
- Ability to work effectively through ambiguity and change management.
