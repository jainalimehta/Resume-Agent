## Shape the Future of Mobility in Ontario

The System Optimization Policy Branch develops tools and policies that maximize the efficiency and effectiveness of Ontario's transportation system. Its work integrates transportation and land use, optimizes infrastructure investments, and provides actionable insights for government, businesses, and the public. The branch aims to reduce congestion, improve mobility, and ensure Ontario's transportation system works better for everyone.
