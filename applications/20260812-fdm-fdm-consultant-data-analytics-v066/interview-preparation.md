# FDM Consultant — Data & Analytics Interview Preparation

## Before You Apply: Confirm These Conditions

Answer these truthfully for yourself. Do not submit or accept terms you cannot meet.

1. Are you currently legally eligible to work in Canada, and what document or status supports that answer?
2. Are you willing and able to commit to at least two years of employment with FDM after training?
3. Can you accept on-site client assignments, potentially in Toronto, Montreal, Halifax, Waterloo, or Calgary?
4. Are you comfortable with the advertised training rate of $18.35/hour and client-assignment salary of $50,000?

## 60-Second Introduction

“I am an early-career Business Analytics graduate living in Toronto. At AYLA Solutions in Australia, I gathered and documented business and reporting requirements, supported SQL extraction and validation, prepared advanced Excel reports, contributed dashboard insights, and participated in Agile planning and reviews. My published portfolio extends that foundation across e-commerce, healthcare, and workforce analytics through PostgreSQL modelling, integrity rules, CTEs, window functions, repeatable validation, AI-assisted interpretation, and Power BI dashboard planning. I am interested in FDM because structured coaching and varied client assignments would let me build on that foundation while becoming a dependable technology consultant.”

## Why FDM?

Use a specific answer:

“FDM appeals to me because the model combines structured upskilling, practice-based learning, ongoing coaching, and real client delivery. I already have a foundation in SQL, analytics, requirements, validation, and communication. I want an environment where feedback is frequent, standards are clear, and I can develop broader delivery capability across industries. The Data Analyst and BI pathways are the closest immediate match, but I am open to learning the technologies demanded by client assignments.”

## Four Stories to Prepare

1. **SQL and data quality:** E-Commerce Sales Analytics — explain schema relationships, integrity rules, recognized revenue, edge cases, and repeatable checks.
2. **Complex operational analysis:** Healthcare Patient & Hospital Analytics — explain how related tables supported completion, cost, workload, billing, and claims analysis.
3. **Requirements and teamwork:** AYLA Solutions — explain how you gathered and documented business/reporting needs and participated in Agile planning and reviews.
4. **Communication and coordination:** Trans Globe Education — explain accurate records, client communication, and process updates across students and institutions.

## Technical Questions to Expect

### SQL

- Explain `INNER JOIN` versus `LEFT JOIN` and when unmatched rows matter.
- Explain CTEs, window functions, `ROW_NUMBER`, `RANK`, running totals, and `LAG`.
- Explain primary keys, foreign keys, uniqueness, null handling, and check constraints.
- Explain how you would investigate duplicates, missing data, inconsistent categories, and unexpected totals.

### Analytics and BI

- Start with the business decision, define the KPI precisely, identify required data, validate logic, choose a useful visual, and explain limitations.
- Be ready to discuss why a metric can be technically correct but operationally misleading.
- Describe Power BI accurately as KPI, visualization, and dashboard planning; do not claim published interactive dashboards.

### Consulting Behaviour

- Clarify ambiguous requests before building.
- Communicate progress and risks early.
- Adapt explanations to technical and non-technical audiences.
- Ask for feedback, document decisions, validate outputs, and remain accountable for follow-through.

## Honest Gap Answers

**“Are you a Data Engineer?”**

“My current hands-on foundation is relational modelling, SQL, integrity rules, data cleaning, and validation. I have not built large-scale production pipelines, so I would not call myself a production Data Engineer yet. I am interested in developing ingestion, transformation, orchestration, cloud, and monitoring skills through structured training.”

**“Have you developed AI applications?”**

“I have used generative AI to support interpretation and recommendations grounded in verified SQL outputs. I have not built a production generative-AI application or framework integration. I can explain how I checked the analysis against source results and would be keen to learn application development responsibly.”

**“How strong is your Power BI?”**

“My verified work covers KPI definition, visualization requirements, and dashboard planning. Interactive dashboard implementation is the next stage, and I do not claim published `.pbix` files. I can defend the business questions, data requirements, measures, visual choices, and validation approach behind each plan.”

## Strong Questions for FDM

1. How are consultants matched to Data Analyst, BI, Data Engineering, or AI pathways after assessment and training?
2. Which technical and consulting competencies determine whether someone is assignment-ready?
3. How much visibility do consultants receive into client location, role scope, and technology before assignment?
4. What support is available when an assignment requires a technology not covered deeply during initial training?
5. How are performance, progression, and mobility managed during the two-year commitment?

## Interview Guardrails

- Do not claim Canadian work eligibility until you can state and document it accurately.
- Do not promise nationwide relocation or a two-year commitment unless you have genuinely decided you can meet those conditions.
- Say `Power BI dashboard planning`, not `Power BI dashboard development`.
- Say `AI-assisted analysis`, not `AI Developer`.
- Say `relational modelling and validation`, not `production data engineering`.
- Describe AYLA as `Data Intern`, never Data Analyst or Business Analyst Intern.
