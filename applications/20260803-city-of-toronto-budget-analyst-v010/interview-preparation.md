## Likely Interview Questions

### Why apply without municipal budget experience?

Be direct. Your evidence is analytical rather than formal budgeting: advanced Excel reporting, SQL extraction, financial and workforce KPI projects, validation, an Integrated MBA, and NISM foundation. Explain your plan to learn City budgeting, GAAP, SAP/S4, Ariba, variance forecasting, and relevant controls. Do not present project revenue analysis as operating-budget experience.

### How would you prepare a monthly variance report?

State this as your learned method: confirm approved budget and forecast versions; obtain actuals, commitments, encumbrances, complement/payroll, and operational drivers; map accounts and cost centres; validate completeness and period cut-off; calculate dollar and percentage variances; separate timing from permanent variances; investigate material items with owners; update full-year forecasts; document assumptions, risks, and corrective actions; reconcile to the source system; and prepare an executive summary.

### Explain favourable and unfavourable variances.

For expenses, actual spending below budget is generally favourable and above budget is unfavourable. For revenue, actual revenue above budget is generally favourable and below budget is unfavourable. Context matters: underspending can reflect vacancies, delays, or unmet service delivery, so financial direction alone does not establish operational success.

### How would you forecast year-end results?

Start with year-to-date actuals and commitments, then project remaining periods using known contracts, payroll/complement, seasonality, run rates, service volumes, approved changes, one-time items, inflation, and management plans. Build base, upside, and downside scenarios where useful; document assumptions; avoid double counting; compare forecast versions; and reconcile totals.

### How would you review a purchase requisition?

Confirm business purpose, authorization, available budget, correct fund/cost centre/account/project code, quantity, unit price, taxes, total calculation, procurement method, supporting quotation or contract, segregation of duties, policy compliance, and duplicate risk. Escalate exceptions rather than changing codes without authority.

### How do you ensure accuracy in financial reports?

Use controlled source data, versioned files, documented definitions, account mapping, reconciliation, control totals, variance thresholds, duplicate and missing-record checks, formula review, reasonableness comparisons, supporting schedules, reviewer sign-off, and a clear audit trail.

### How would you explain a variance to senior management?

Lead with the decision-relevant message: amount and direction, cause, whether timing or permanent, service impact, year-end forecast, risk, actions underway, decision required, and owner. Use plain language and place detailed account schedules in an appendix.

### Tell us about handling confidential data.

Use Arihant Investment. Explain the real records involved at a safe level, how you maintained accuracy and confidentiality, restricted unnecessary disclosure, verified information, and communicated carefully. Do not invent a security system or incident.

## Budget and Accounting Knowledge to Build

### Operating versus Capital Budget

- **Operating:** recurring service-delivery revenues and expenses such as salaries, supplies, fuel, repairs, contracts, and routine program costs.
- **Capital:** acquisition, construction, or major improvement of long-lived assets and multi-year projects.
- Understand approved budget, actuals, commitments, forecast, variance, carry-forward, reserve/reserve fund, debt, grant/subsidy, and year-end close concepts.

### Core Accounting

- Accounting equation; debit and credit fundamentals.
- Accounts receivable, accounts payable, general ledger, subledgers, chart of accounts, journal entries, accruals, prepaids, deferrals, depreciation/amortization, receivable aging, provisions, and account reconciliation.
- Financial statements: statement of financial position, statement of operations, cash flow, and changes in net financial assets or debt in a public-sector context.
- Learn Canadian public-sector accounting standards and how they differ from private-sector terminology; do not claim GAAP expertise before preparation.

### Budget Controls

- Approved authority, account/fund/cost-centre/project coding, commitment control, requisition and purchase-order workflow, segregation of duties, approval limits, supporting documentation, change control, budget transfers, and audit trail.

### Complement and Payroll

- Approved positions, filled/vacant status, full-time equivalents, start/end dates, salary progression, benefits, overtime, premiums, acting assignments, vacancies, gapping assumptions, collective-agreement impacts, and forecast reconciliation to payroll actuals.

### Capital Project Tracking

- Approved budget, prior-year actuals, current-year actuals, commitments, forecast-to-complete, total projected cost, completion percentage, schedule, cash flow, variance, change orders, risks, funding source, and year-end/carry-forward implications.
