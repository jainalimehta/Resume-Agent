## Role

CIBC Capital Markets serves corporate, government, and institutional clients across capital raising, investing, and related financial solutions. The Equities team provides institutional investors with original research ideas, service, and execution support across North America.

The Associate supports the Research Analyst covering the Information Technology sector.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct company and industry research, analyze trends, and develop knowledge of financial markets, financial statements, and valuation drivers.
- Build, maintain, and enhance financial models, databases, and analytical tools supporting research coverage and investment insights.
- Prepare company reports, thematic research, and written commentary for institutional clients.
- Participate in calls, meetings, and industry events with public-company management teams.
- Collaborate with Sales, Trading, Investment Banking, and clients to support the research franchise.
