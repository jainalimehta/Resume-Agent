# Junior Sales Representative

**Company:** VV Vantage Consulting  
**Location:** 936 The East Mall, Etobicoke, ON M9B 6J9  
**Employment:** Permanent, Full-time; in person  
**Pay:** $42,000–$54,000 per year

We are seeking a Junior Sales Representative to support our continued expansion in Etobicoke, ON. Our organization is a performance-driven, team-focused firm, and we are proud to have been entrusted with introducing the newest product innovations from a leading telecommunications provider.

In this role, you will contribute directly to the customer experience by fostering strong relationships, ensuring service excellence, and supporting the successful rollout of new telecommunications offerings. Your work will play an important part in maintaining customer satisfaction, strengthening brand presence, and upholding key performance targets. The ideal candidate is professional, articulate, and committed to delivering results that reflect both our firm’s standards and our client’s expectations.

## Key Responsibilities

- Deliver professional customer service while introducing telecommunications products and solutions.
- Build trust and rapport through clear, customer-focused communication.
- Guide customers through product selection and support a seamless service process.
- Assist with the launch and promotion of new telecom services within the Etobicoke market.
- Maintain accurate customer records and uphold service quality standards.
- Support customer retention and overall brand performance.
- Represent both organizations with professionalism and integrity.

## Qualifications

- 1–3 years of experience in customer service, hospitality, retail, or sales is an asset; however, full paid training is provided and the company will invest in the right person.
- Strong verbal communication skills and confidence in customer interactions.
- A customer-first mindset and the ability to adapt to a fast-paced environment.
- Willingness to learn and stay informed on product updates and new offerings.
- Reliability, strong work ethic, and attention to detail.

This is a hands-on, in-person role in Etobicoke, where you’ll engage directly with customers while collaborating with a motivated, results-driven team.

- Additional earning opportunities tied to service quality and customer satisfaction.

If you are seeking a long-term role with growth potential and the opportunity to contribute to a high-performing team, we encourage you to apply. We look forward to meeting candidates who are ready to support our expansion across Etobicoke, ON and deliver exceptional service.
