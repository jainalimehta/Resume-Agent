# Data Analyst

Company: SOCAN

Location: Toronto, ON or Montreal, QC

Position type: Permanent Full-Time

Work model: Hybrid

Hiring range: $71,920 to $89,900

Language: English required; French is an asset

## Organization

SOCAN is a not-for-profit copyright collective that helps music creators and publishers receive compensation for the use of their work through licensing, global royalty collection, and advocacy. SOCAN supports nearly 200,000 songwriters, composers, and music publishers and is committed to diversity, equity, inclusion, and anti-racism.

## Role

The Data Analyst joins the Analytics department and turns raw data into actionable business insights through reports and dashboards that improve organizational initiatives. The role works closely with data engineers, BI analysts, other data analysts, and business leaders and requires curiosity, continuous learning, and interest in modern cloud data stacks.

## Responsibilities

- Document reporting requirements and understand how business processes affect data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis to clean, transform, and analyze large datasets in Databricks, identifying trends, anomalies, and improvement opportunities.
- Create data-quality tests and plans for reporting and Lakehouse projects and help troubleshoot discrepancies and pipeline issues.
- Work with department leaders to define and document KPIs.
- Perform root-cause analysis on KPI fluctuations and provide actionable insights.
- Collaborate on interactive Databricks AI/BI dashboards and reports for varied stakeholders.
- Curate and document semantic layers supporting natural-language queries through Genie.
- Participate in daily stand-ups, planning sessions, and retrospectives.
- Prioritize and respond to ad hoc analysis requests.

## Qualifications

- Strong analytical and quantitative skills with large datasets and a big-picture perspective.
- Strong SQL and intermediate Python.
- Understanding of Spark and distributed computing.
- At least two years of data analysis experience using Databricks or similar technologies.
- Strong communication, solution-oriented problem solving, business writing, and technical documentation.
- Degree in a quantitative field such as Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, or Business Analytics, or equivalent experience.
- French and a passion for music are assets.

Competencies include critical thinking and problem solving, emotional intelligence, commitment to continuous learning, and a process-oriented approach.
