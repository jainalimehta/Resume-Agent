## What is the Opportunity?

In the increasingly complex world of financial services, accurate and complete data is critical for risk management, financial reporting and regulatory compliance. Ensuring data governance begins with an understanding of financial services data, identifying the critical elements (CDEs), data quality controls and resolving data issues.

As a Data Prioritization Analyst with the data prioritization team, you will be accountable for working with various data domains to prioritize critical data associated with business processes. Reporting to the Associate Director, you will work in collaboration with other teams providing data services such as the DMOs, Data Lineage, Data Quality, Metadata Management, and Data Completeness Teams.

## What will you do?

### Data Prioritization

- Create and support a repeatable, robust process to identify data elements that are most critical and have the highest impact on RBC's key processes. Seek out industry trends and organizational knowledge to understand alternative approaches and propose viable options.
- Engage Data Domain stewards and Business Unit leadership to identify and define key business processes and prioritize and identify critical reports.
- Engage Report Owners and their delegates to prioritize data using the enterprise-approved Critical Data Element identification methodology.
- Support other Data Management activities.
- Identify, capture and curate metadata associated with CDEs, such as Business Term, Data Classification, Owning Data Domain and vintage tags, for U.S. key regulatory reports.
- Maintain IHC Regulatory Reporting Data Domain metadata associated with Data Prioritization and CDEs in Collibra, RBC's Enterprise Data Governance Platform.

### Communication and Documentation

- Document critical data, its usage in business processes and reports, and the rationale for critical data in the firm's standard CDE memo template.
- Engage stakeholders from Risk Management, Finance, Liquidity, AML and other business units.
- Manage and report work status, including escalating execution risks in a timely manner.

### Training and Continuous Improvement

- Gain and apply an in-depth understanding of data management, data governance, and metadata tools and systems to support the definition and documentation of effective data elements.
- Work with the Chief Data Office and other Data Management Offices to support standards and best practices to improve data quality, including Data Working Groups and Data Literacy projects.
