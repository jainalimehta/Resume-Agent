# PointClickCare Interview Defense Guide

## Confirm before applying

- Verify the exact job title on the official posting; “Contract Operations Analyst” is inferred from the duties.
- Verify the listed work location and the frequency of required Mississauga office attendance.
- Do not claim CLM, CRM, contract interpretation, legal operations, or high-volume contract-review experience.

## How to answer the experience gap

“I have not yet used a CLM or CRM platform or interpreted legal contract terms. My closest evidence is accurate confidential record management, SQL and Excel validation, requirements documentation, and structured integrity rules in relational projects. I would learn the approved templates, playbooks, metadata definitions, escalation paths, and system workflow before working independently. I would ask questions when a term is ambiguous and never substitute my judgment for Legal.”

## Contract-review approach

If asked how you would review an agreement, describe a controlled process rather than claiming experience:

1. Confirm the correct template, agreement type, counterparty, version, and required approvals.
2. Check mandatory fields, dates, entities, signatures, exhibits, amendments, and internal consistency.
3. Compare deviations against the approved playbook without offering legal interpretation.
4. Record metadata exactly in the CLM/CRM and reconcile it to the source agreement.
5. Flag exceptions with a clear description and route them to the authorized Legal or Compliance owner.
6. Perform a final quality check and preserve the approved document and correspondence according to policy.

## Responsible AI answer

“In my portfolio, I used AI to assist with interpreting analytical outputs, but I grounded the findings in SQL results and validation checks. In contract operations, I would use only company-approved tools and permitted data. I would protect confidential and privileged information, verify extracted fields and summaries against the source agreement, document exceptions, and keep a human decision-maker responsible for the final review.”

## Likely technical questions

- What makes data trustworthy? Clear field definitions, required-field checks, valid formats, uniqueness, referential integrity, reconciliation to source documents, exception handling, ownership, and periodic validation.
- How would you prioritize competing requests? Confirm deadline and business risk, separate urgent signature blockers from routine updates, clarify dependencies, maintain a visible queue, communicate trade-offs early, and complete a quality check before closure.
- What would you report? Intake volume, status, turnaround time, aging, missing fields, exception categories, recurring discrepancies, and reconciliation results—but only after definitions and privacy rules are approved.
- How would you learn a CLM? Study the data dictionary and process map, observe controlled examples, practice in a safe environment, use checklists, validate against source agreements, request feedback, and document approved procedures.

## Questions to ask PointClickCare

1. What is the exact title and which team owns the role day to day?
2. Which CLM and CRM platforms does the team use, and what does onboarding look like for a new user?
3. Which contract types and metadata fields create the most frequent quality issues?
4. How are review authority and escalation divided between this role and Legal or Compliance?
5. What accuracy, turnaround, backlog, or data-quality measures define success in the first 90 days?
6. Which AI use cases are approved, and what privacy, privilege, and human-review controls govern them?
7. What is the expected Mississauga office cadence for this hybrid position?

## Non-negotiable guardrails

- Say “portfolio project,” not client or production healthcare system.
- Say “Power BI planning,” not implemented dashboard.
- Say “AI-assisted findings verified against SQL outputs,” not automated legal review.
- Never invent metrics, contract volumes, CLM/CRM names, legal knowledge, compliance decisions, or Canadian work experience.
