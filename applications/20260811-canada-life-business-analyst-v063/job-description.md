# Business Analyst

Temporary Full Time

The Business Analyst plays an important role that supports business acquisition. The role is responsible for delivering data and reporting, identifying and realizing efficiencies and supporting the analysis of business requirements, processes, and workflows.

This role combines technical knowledge of the various systems/data and a business understanding of what those details mean in order to build and maintain effective ways of working and support strategic initiatives.

## What You Will Do

- Build, manage and maintain reporting, dashboards and automations.
- Guide and facilitate the analysis, requirements gathering and scoping for business initiatives or projects that reflect the needs of business.
- Partner with stakeholders to understand business processes and needs to elicit, capture, validate, and prioritize business requirements.
- Collaborate with business partners to ensure the system solution matches all business requirements.
- Work with project team members to ensure testing and test strategy adequately supports requirements for product and system changes.
- Communicate effectively with project, production, and leadership teams regarding problems, solutions, and the rationale for both.
- Provide updates on project status, timelines, risks, issues, and costs.
- Contribute to training strategies and business implementation plans.

## What You Will Bring

- Post-secondary diploma or degree, or equivalent experience, in a relevant field such as Finance or Data Science.
- Knowledge of financial products, systems, and the business.
- Critical thinking, attention to detail, and a high degree of accuracy.
- Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work in a rapidly changing environment while understanding responsible change management.
- Ability to prioritize multiple projects using strong time and task management.
- Self-starter who is innovative, open to learning, and accepting of new ideas.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office, with emphasis on Excel.
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Power BI and automation tools such as Power Query and Power Automate.
- Experience with data analytics tools such as SQL, Python, or R is an asset.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Business Analyst certification is an asset.
- Bilingualism in French and English is an asset and is required for Quebec only.
- Reliability Status Security Clearance is required as a condition of employment after an offer.

The base salary is between $60,000 and $89,900 annually. The position is posted in several locations. Canada Life is committed to improving the financial, physical, and mental well-being of Canadians and values people who step up, do the right thing, and deliver.
