# Junior Data Analyst

**Company:** AspiringIT

**Location:** Canada

**Employment:** Full-time, Remote, Entry Level

**Experience:** 1+ years

AspiringIT is seeking a detail-oriented Junior Data Analyst to support data-driven decision-making across the organization. The ideal candidate will have experience analyzing data, creating actionable insights, and developing Power BI dashboards and DAX reports that help stakeholders track business performance.

## Responsibilities

- Collect, clean, validate, and analyze data from multiple sources.
- Design, develop, and maintain Power BI dashboards and reports.
- Create and optimize DAX measures, calculated columns, and KPIs.
- Generate insights and recommendations based on data trends and business requirements.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define reporting needs and deliver analytical solutions.
- Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and reporting quality.

## Required Qualifications

- 1+ years of experience in Data Analysis, Business Intelligence, or a related role.
- Hands-on experience with Power BI Dashboard Development and DAX Reporting.
- Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel and data analysis techniques.
- Knowledge of SQL for data extraction and manipulation.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently in a remote environment.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with data visualization best practices.
- Familiarity with ETL processes and data modeling.
- Exposure to cloud-based analytics platforms is a plus.
