# Program Coordinator, International Student Development Programs

- University of Toronto, Centre for International Experience, Vice-Provost, Students
- Req ID: 49605; Position: 00050241
- St. George Campus, Toronto

- Closing: August 7, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET

## Role focus

Develop and deliver programs, resources, events, and educational workshops supporting international students across transition in, journey years, and transition out. Work across Student Life, tri-campus international centres, faculties, colleges, clubs, and student groups. Maintain accessible, holistic programming and current website, social, and digital communications. Evaluate program impact and relevance.

## Responsibilities

- Plan and implement curricular and co-curricular programs and activities.
- Coordinate event and program logistics.
- Develop promotional content and student outreach strategies.
- Write program and service summary reports.
- Implement program and service process improvements.
- Build collaborative networks and stakeholder capacity.
- Coordinate casual-staff recruitment and training.
- Support program evaluation, assessment tools, and an ISE evaluation framework.

## Essential qualifications

- Bachelor's degree or equivalent education and experience.
- Four years designing, delivering, and evaluating international-student experience programs, including three years each in international-student development, collaborative programming, and event coordination.
- Knowledge of international-student transitions, distributed student supports, and the student life cycle.
- Intercultural competency; experience with diverse communities; anti-oppression and equity practices.
- Team-based program development, leadership of large project components, and strong interpersonal and intercultural communication.
- Strong administration, word processing, data analysis, desktop publishing, and databases.
- Assessment-tool development and quantitative and qualitative program evaluation.
- Flexibility, comfort with uncertainty, and accuracy.

## Assets and attributes

- University faculty, staff, and student experience.
- International experience in a non-Canadian education setting.
- Project-management training.
- Approachable, diplomatic, proactive communicator, problem solver, and team player.
