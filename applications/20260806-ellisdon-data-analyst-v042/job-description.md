You As a Data Analyst Will

Elicit, analyze, specify, and validate the business needs of internal and external stakeholders;
Leverage your: knowledge of statistical/mathematical methods; expertise with data analysis tools and techniques; and advanced analytical ability to derive actionable insights from data;
Create informative visualizations (dashboards) and reports that intuitively displays large amounts of data and/or complex relationships;
Determine, implement, and evaluate business metrics to meet ongoing organizational business objectives;
Work closely with scrum development teams to maximize the value of data products;
Identify new sources of data and develop data collection systems to improve data collection, analysis, and reporting;
Support data migrations from legacy systems into new systems such as Workday, Financial systems, ..etc

Is this the right role for you?

2-4 years of relevant work experience as an analyst or equivalent
Experience solving analytical problems using quantitative approaches
Construction experience working on (50M+) Civil or Building projects an asset
Knowledge and experience in the following:
Business Intelligence Tools (experience with DundasBI an asset);
Databases (SQL, NoSQL, relational, etc.);
Statistical analysis (Excel, SPSS, SAS, R, or Python);
ETL methodologies and toolsets;
Scripting language an asset (Python, or other).
