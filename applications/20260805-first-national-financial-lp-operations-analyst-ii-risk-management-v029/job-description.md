## The Opportunity

Reporting to Operations Manager, Risk Management & Internal Audit, the Operations Analyst provides support to the Risk Management & Internal Audit department by contributing to the completion of project deliverables and is accountable for the analysis, reporting and support of internal applications.

## How You Will Contribute

- Monitor and support the existing QA Tool system for supported lines of business.
- Maintain an up-to-date residential underwriting employee directory and communicate with Residential Underwriting Management and HR to ensure accuracy of employee status.
- Create Risk Management audit data samples for all lines of business within the Risk Management audit-program criteria.
- Assign and distribute data samples to QA Specialists according to the QA sampling and assignment methodology.
- Complete reviews to identify potential internal fraud by monitoring segregation of duties within residential underwriting.
- Publish quarterly data extractions for National QA Audit results for supported lines of business.
- Participate in weekly departmental reporting.
- Analyze business requirements and help design solutions based on the technology, infrastructure, and timelines provided.
- Develop solutions using appropriate Microsoft technologies, with heavy emphasis on Visual Basic and SQL Server.
- Work with the Manager, Risk Management & Internal Audit, key stakeholders, and end users to ensure quality, accuracy, and correct business solutions.
- Assist in developing processes and technologies used by QA, Fraud, Risk Management, and Internal Audit teams.
- Apply analytical thinking and problem solving to operationalize new processes and technologies.
- Communicate with stakeholders and management in meetings and by email.
- Make fact-based decisions involving business requirements, sampling methodology, root-cause analysis, recommended fixes, and segregation-of-duties risk.
- Work autonomously and bring anomalies to the manager's attention.
- Tailor service delivery to the technical and operational knowledge of the audience and anticipate missing requirements.
