# Business Operations Coordinator

**Department:** Tech

**Location:** Toronto, Ontario (Remote)

## About the Company

Makeship empowers influencers, creators, and brands of all sizes to develop and launch limited-edition products that matter to their fans. Leveraging design, manufacturing, and marketing expertise, Makeship works with partners to bring products to life through a community-powered crowdfunding platform. Each product is given a window of 21 days to be funded by the community before production and worldwide shipping. Makeship guarantees quality and ethical sourcing.

Makeship is profitable, has three-year revenue growth of over 200%, ranked 97th in 2024 and 86th on The Globe and Mail's Canada's Top Growing Companies of 2025 list, and exceeded $65M CAD in sales during 2025. Makeship has been Great Place to Work-Certified for three years in a row.

## About the Role

As a member of the Technology and Operations team, the Business Operations Coordinator will help shape Makeship's culture, processes, and future while collaborating across the content-creation business.

Makeship is looking for a Business Operations Coordinator to act as the backbone of internal operations. This highly collaborative, people-oriented role requires fast and reliable responses to urgent needs as well as thoughtful support for longer-term operational planning. The coordinator will work with teams across product design, sales, finance, marketing, and engineering to solve problems and keep operations effective.

The Business Operations Coordinator will work directly with data and low-code/no-code systems, progressing from understanding and troubleshooting to modifying and improving workflows and databases.

## Key Responsibilities

- Coordinate with cross-departmental stakeholders to move projects and events forward.
- Coordinate campaign execution, ensuring products launch as expected on makeship.com, manufacturing partners are prepared for production, and related steps are completed.
- Act as the primary point of contact and support for internal operations.
- Document internal systems, processes, and standard operating procedures.
- Support coordination, design, and implementation of business workflows.
- Support debugging, optimization, and building of low-code/no-code automations.
- Support ad-hoc reporting, data analysis, and data-quality initiatives.

## What You Bring

- 2+ years of experience coordinating projects across multiple teams and stakeholders.
- 1+ years of experience with Shopify or another e-commerce platform.
- 1+ years of experience with low-code/no-code automation platforms such as Airtable or Zapier.
- 1+ years of experience reporting and analyzing data from Shopify, HubSpot, and/or databases such as Airtable or BigQuery.
- Excellent written and visual process communication using tools such as Miro or Figma.
- Courage to learn new systems, technologies, and processes on the fly.
- Ability to switch contexts rapidly while keeping tasks and information organized.
- User-focused, creative, solutions-oriented mindset.

## Bonus

- CAPM certification.
- Startup experience.
- Experience using AI such as Claude to automate workflows.

## Benefits and Compensation

Remote work anywhere in Canada and/or access to Makeship hubs. Salary band is $55,000–$65,000 CAD per annum. This posting represents an existing vacancy.
