## Likely Questions and Answer Direction

### Why this role when your experience is early career?

Be direct: the posting requests expertise in any of several areas, and your strongest fit is analytics, reporting, research, requirements documentation, and Generative AI-assisted analysis. Emphasize graduate training, internship evidence, portfolio depth, learning speed, and public-service motivation. Do not claim senior-level leadership you have not held.

### Describe a digital-transformation opportunity.

Use a simple structure: understand the user journey; map the current process; identify pain points and accessibility needs; define measurable outcomes; validate data and requirements; evaluate options and risks; prototype; conduct UAT; train users; monitor adoption and KPIs. Clearly label this as your approach, not past ownership.

### How do you ensure accurate reporting?

Discuss business definitions, source-to-report traceability, null and duplicate checks, referential integrity, reconciliation, exception review, repeatable SQL, peer or stakeholder validation, version control, and documented assumptions.

### How have you used Generative AI?

Explain that AI-assisted sections are completed in your three GitHub projects. Describe structured prompts for interpretation and recommendations, validating outputs against SQL evidence, documenting assumptions, and retaining human judgement. Be clear that this is Generative AI-assisted analysis—not a production agentic-AI or RPA implementation.

### How would you handle an unfamiliar policy or legislation?

Explain that you would identify authoritative Ontario and ministry sources, consult policy and legal subject-matter experts, document interpretations and open questions, translate confirmed rules into requirements, maintain traceability, and never make an unsupported policy assumption.

### Tell us about stakeholder disagreement.

Use an actual AYLA, Trans Globe, or Arihant example if you remember one. Explain how you clarified the shared outcome, separated facts from assumptions, documented requirements or options, confirmed decisions, and followed up. Do not invent a conflict.

### How would you plan user acceptance testing?

State this as working knowledge: trace test cases to approved requirements, cover normal and exception scenarios, define expected results and acceptance criteria, prepare representative data, record defects with severity and evidence, retest fixes, obtain business sign-off, and preserve an audit trail.
