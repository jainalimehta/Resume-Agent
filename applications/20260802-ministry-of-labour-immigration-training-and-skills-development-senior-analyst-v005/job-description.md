## Opportunity

The Labour Relations Solutions Division seeks performance-driven and innovative professionals with expertise in one or more of these areas:

- Low-code software such as Salesforce and/or web development
- Data analytics, reporting, transformation, and ETL
- Digital and business-process transformation using agentic AI or robotic process automation
- Project management using Agile, Scrum, and Lean
- UX/content design and technical writing

## Responsibilities

- Drive user-centred digital-transformation initiatives.
- Develop innovative solutions that optimize client experience and business processes.
- Provide reporting solutions, analytical expertise, and technical leadership for data mining and reporting initiatives within the ministry, across the OPS, and for external stakeholders.
- Provide project leadership and expertise in planning and executing the design, analysis, implementation, and reporting of client-facing labour-relations services and program data.
- Lead user research and user-experience sessions supporting client-centred public-facing digital solutions.

## Technical knowledge and skills

- I&IT technologies and trends, databases, object-oriented and web programming.
- Low-code/no-code platforms, business-intelligence and business-analysis tools and methodologies, and data stores.
- Business-process design using automation toolsets and process-improvement methodologies.
- Generative artificial intelligence, machine learning, and/or robotic process automation.

## Analytical and research skills

- Quantitative and qualitative research and analysis methods using computer systems and software.
- Trend analysis, system modelling, and evaluation of best practices, options, costs, and impacts.
- Research theory and methodology and statistical-analysis techniques to design, conduct, and consult on research and interpret statistical information.
- Program-evaluation design and accurate performance-data reporting.

## Project management and leadership

- Plan and lead complex, business-led digital-service-delivery and data-reporting projects.
- Provide technical guidance on analytical methodologies and resolve technical issues.
- Coordinate management, consultants, stakeholders, clients, and a team of coordinators for provincial implementation.
- Advise senior management and stakeholders on project status, business processes, and change management.

## Relationship management and communication

- Develop functional business requirements, high-level project plans, and business-process maps.
- Present technical business proposals to senior management.
- Draft AODA-compliant communications and content for senior management, internal staff, and external stakeholders.
- Coordinate and host business-analysis, project-development, and implementation meetings.
- Conduct virtual and in-person presentations to external stakeholders promoting ministry labour-relations services.
- Liaise with I&IT regarding business requirements, user-acceptance testing, and deployment.

## Business knowledge

- Provincial labour-relations legislation and other jurisdictions' labour legislation and its application.
- Provincial and ministry strategic direction and financial, administrative, and procurement policies.
- Government decision-making and accountability frameworks, including central agencies, business planning, program monitoring and reporting, and allocation processes.
