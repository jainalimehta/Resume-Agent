# Associate IT Business Systems Analyst

We are currently recruiting for an Associate IT Business Systems Analyst to support our **IT Member Services** team.

This role can work out of our Toronto or Burlington offices.

## Position Overview

The **Associate IT Business Systems Analyst** role serves as the bridge between business challenges and technological solutions. This role requires an adept understanding of business needs, analyzing processes, and designing systems that enhance productivity, efficiency, and overall business performance, while having a keen eye for detail and a strong grasp of both technical and business domains.

They are responsible for ensuring that business requirements are effectively translated into workable technological solutions and critical to ensuring that technology investments deliver the intended business value to MMC Customers and support MMC's organizational goals.

## Key Responsibilities

- Participates in small and medium-sized IT Business system initiatives in areas of responsibility.
- Gathering and documenting business requirements from stakeholders.
- Participate in meetings with project teams and stakeholders.
- Provide end user support to optimize IT business solutions.
- Provide quality assurance of documentation, testing, configuration and development completed by third parties.
- Provide ongoing support and maintenance for implemented systems, troubleshoot issues, and make necessary adjustments or upgrades.

## Qualifications and Skills

- High-quality written and verbal communication skills
- Strong interpersonal skills along with an attention to detail, including the ability to prepare and deliver process documentation, specifications, etc.
- Demonstrated commitment to exceptional customer service
- Work independently, setting goals, and meeting timelines
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Ability to balance multiple work demands in a fast-paced environment with frequently changing priorities

## Education, Training, and Experience

### Mandatory

- College Diploma in IT, Computer Science, Business Analysis, Business or equivalent experience
- 1-3 years’ of progressive work experience in Information Systems and Technology or similar team
- Excellent working knowledge of the Microsoft Office tools suite.
- Working towards Business Analyst or Project Management certification/Designation
- Experienced in providing business end user support.
- Ability to work with multi-functional teams.

### Preferred

- Experience working in supply chain and/or non-public sector organization.
- University Degree in IT, Computer Science, Business Analysis.

## About Us

Mohawk Medbuy Corporation (MMC) is a national, not-for-profit, shared services organization that supports hundreds of health care providers across Canada, as well as child welfare agencies and other public sector organizations. MMC provides value-driven contracting and procurement solutions for medical/surgical supplies, pharmaceutical products, local sourcing, capital (equipment, FF&E and redevelopment) and nutrition solutions. Other services include data analytics, in-hospital logistics, warehousing, technology, procure-to-pay and accounts payable.

With an emerging focus on sustainability and reconciliation with Indigenous Peoples, MMC is actively supporting the creation of a resilient value chain that incorporates environmental, social and governance best practices. Based in Burlington, ON, the organization also has offices in Toronto, Oakville, Thunder Bay, Kingston, Brantford, London and Chatham, and a distribution centre in Oakville. Learn more: [www.mohawkmedbuy.ca](http://www.mohawkmedbuy.ca).
