## Before You Apply

- Decide whether you can travel to MMC's Toronto, Burlington, or other offices when required.
- Review the posting's vaccination condition privately and confirm whether you can satisfy it. Do not disclose medical details beyond what the authorized hiring process requires.

## 60-Second Introduction

At AYLA Solutions in Australia, I gathered and documented business and reporting requirements, supported SQL extraction and validation, prepared advanced Excel reports, contributed dashboard insights, and participated in Agile planning and reviews. My portfolio extends that foundation through relational modelling, integrity rules, repeatable validation, GitHub Actions checks, and clear analytical documentation. ## Four Stories to Prepare

1. **Requirements and documentation:** AYLA — describe the business/reporting need, questions asked, documentation produced, and how the analytical work stayed aligned.
2. **Quality and exception handling:** E-Commerce Sales Analytics — explain primary/foreign keys, uniqueness and value checks, recognized revenue, and repeatable validation.
3. **Connected health care data:** Healthcare Patient & Hospital Analytics — explain the table relationships and how you evaluated completion, costs, billing, workload, and claims.
4. **Customer support and coordination:** Trans Globe — explain accurate records, communication with students and institutions, and how you handled process updates.

## Requirements-to-Solution Framework

1. Clarify the business outcome, affected users, current process, pain points, and decision owner.
2. Identify functional requirements, data requirements, business rules, exceptions, access needs, and constraints.
3. Separate the underlying need from a stakeholder's proposed solution.
4. Define acceptance criteria and trace each requirement to testing and business value.
5. Validate the documented understanding with stakeholders and maintain decisions through change control.
6. Support delivery by answering questions, reviewing evidence, tracking issues, and confirming outcomes with users.

## End-User Support Framework

When asked how you would handle an issue:

1. Confirm the user, business impact, urgency, affected system, timing, and exact steps.
2. Reproduce the issue safely and gather screenshots, messages, identifiers, logs, and recent changes where appropriate.
3. Check known issues, permissions, data quality, configuration, integrations, and environment differences.
4. Communicate status and workaround clearly without promising an unverified resolution time.
5. Escalate with a complete, reproducible record; test the fix; confirm with the user; document the resolution and prevention action.

## Technical Topics to Review

- Functional versus non-functional requirements
- User stories, use cases, acceptance criteria, and traceability
- Incident versus problem versus change
- Severity versus priority
- UAT, regression testing, defect lifecycle, and sign-off
- Configuration management, environments, releases, rollback, and change control
- Authentication, authorization, least privilege, privacy, and audit trails
- Integration basics: APIs, payloads, field mapping, validation, status codes, and error handling
- Health care data sensitivity and why minimum necessary access, confidentiality, and auditability matter

## Strong Questions for MMC

1. Which Member Services systems and user groups would this analyst support first?
2. How are requirements, incidents, changes, tests, and approvals documented and traced today?
3. What work is handled internally versus by third-party vendors, and how does MMC evaluate vendor deliverables?
4. What does successful support and system health look like during the first 90 days?
5. Which training or BA/PM designation would MMC encourage for an associate analyst?

## Interview Guardrails

- Say `client support and coordination`, not `IT end-user support experience`.
- Say `data validation and automated checks`, not `system QA`.
- Say `portfolio systems and models`, not `production system maintenance`.
- Say `completed Coursera credential`, not `certified Business Analyst`.
- Say `healthcare analytics project`, not health care industry employment.
- Describe AYLA as `Data Intern`, never Business Systems Analyst Intern.
