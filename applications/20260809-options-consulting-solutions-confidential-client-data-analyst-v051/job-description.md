# Data Analyst

Reference: 13997

Recruiter: Rujeeka Manoharan, Options Consulting Solutions

Client: Confidential

Location: Mississauga, Ontario — Hybrid

Term: 12-month contract

Expected compensation: CAD $38–$48 per hour

## Company

The client is described as a dynamic, forward-thinking organization dedicated to empowering professionals with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed. The position offers a hybrid work environment and a collaborative, supportive team.

## Responsibilities

- Coordinate and maintain data and records while creating Excel and Power BI dashboards that establish efficient reporting systems.
- Analyze data to identify trends and support data-driven decisions.
- Create and maintain KPI dashboards, reports, and visualizations in Excel and Power BI.
- Prepare weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual leadership reports.
- Track incidents, corrective actions, audits, inspections, and compliance activities.
- Administer SharePoint sites and improve reporting processes using Power Automate workflows.
- Maintain accurate, complete, timely records and documentation.
- Coordinate stakeholder follow-ups to ensure action items are completed.
- Assist with communications, presentations, and administrative support.
- Identify opportunities to improve reporting efficiency and business processes.

## Qualifications

- Advanced Microsoft Excel, including formulas, pivot tables, charts, and data analysis.
- Post-secondary diploma or degree in Business Administration, Data Analytics, or a related field.
- Two to three years of data coordination, reporting, and administrative-support experience.
- Corporate, industrial, aviation, or another operational environment is preferred.
- Power BI dashboard development and data visualization proficiency is mandatory.
- Strong Microsoft Word, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
- SharePoint document and information management experience.
- Power Automate workflow and automation experience.
- Exceptional analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills with strong attention to detail.
