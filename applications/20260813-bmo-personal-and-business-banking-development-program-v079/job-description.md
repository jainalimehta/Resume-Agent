## Opportunity

BMO's two-year Personal and Business Banking Development Program develops knowledge across Product Management, Risk Management, Data & Analytics, and Collections. Participants work with banking product, analytics, risk, and collections leaders to understand how Personal and Business Banking operates and how its functions interact.

## Rotation areas

- Product Management: develop and improve customer products, differentiated strategies, value propositions, and marketing offers.
- Risk Management: shape risk appetite and lending strategies and controls to optimize return within risk limits.
- Data & Analytics: use analytics to generate insights and support strategic business decisions.
- Collections: transform customer experience and operations through data, analytics, and modelling.

## Accountabilities

- Support product strategies, sales of existing products, new-product launches, profitability, and customer experience.
- Support product-risk management, regulatory compliance, controls, and reporting.
- Apply analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills across Product, Data & Analytics, Risk, and Collections.
- Plan and deploy analytical solutions for issue understanding, insights, decisions, and business outcomes.
- Support data-quality, analytics, reporting, business-intelligence, modelling, and decision-support strategies and roadmaps.
- Design, implement, and manage analytics and reporting solutions.
- Analyze data, diagnose issues, and make business recommendations.
- Produce recurring and ad hoc reports, visualizations, graphics, and dashboards.
- Structure multi-dimensional customer, product, demographic, and transaction data at different granularities.
- Monitor tool performance, support user acceptance testing, and resolve issues.
- Integrate multiple sources to improve analysis, processes, and reporting.
- Document operating procedures and analytical/reporting processes.
- Improve processes and customer experience.
- Support Salesforce training, materials, and change management.
- Identify risks and develop controls and processes.
- Potentially support sales deals, presentations, partners, and vendors.

## Qualifications

- Undergraduate or graduate degree in Business, Commerce, Data & Analytics, Risk Management, Economics, Engineering, Finance, or Mathematics.
- Work experience, including co-op or internships, is highly recommended.
- Technical proficiency through business and work experience is highly recommended.
- Strong data preparation and analysis experience.
- Familiarity with Power BI, TIBCO BPM, Spotfire, Tableau, SQL, SAS, R, Python, MATLAB, or SPSS.
- Business cases and market-trend research.
- Strong written and verbal communication, collaboration, analytical problem-solving, data-driven decision-making, and influence.
