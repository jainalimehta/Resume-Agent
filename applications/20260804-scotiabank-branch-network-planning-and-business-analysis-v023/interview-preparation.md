## Risk and Escalation Framework

When an issue or missing input appears:

1. Verify the facts, source, owner, due date, and latest status.
2. Assess impact on scope, timing, cost, quality, customer experience, compliance, or decision readiness.
3. Distinguish a current issue from a possible future risk.
4. Identify practical options, dependencies, and recommended next step.
5. Escalate early to the appropriate manager with a concise summary and supporting evidence.
6. Record the decision, owner, deadline, and follow-up.

Do not claim knowledge of Scotiabank's internal risk appetite. State that you would learn and apply the Bank's policies, controls, Code of Conduct, and escalation expectations.
