## Program

Mastercard's 18-month Launch Program combines hands-on experience, structured learning, mentorship, cross-functional collaboration, and technical, analytical, and professional development.

## Associate Consultant responsibilities

- Collaborate in small teams to help major brands answer and implement solutions for strategic questions.
- Own deliverables and build external client relationships.
- Apply technology and data across financial services, retail, restaurants, hospitality, and other industries.

## Business Experimentation track

- Use Mastercard's Test & Learn predictive-analytics platform and SQL to run data-backed business experiments.
- Develop data-science-based insights for product launches, marketing campaigns, customer loyalty, and pricing.
- Share findings with clients and help determine which ideas work and where, when, and how to implement them.
- Typical workload: two to four concurrent clients in long-term engagements of one to two years.

## Strategy & Transformation track

- Guide clients through strategic, tactical, operational, and transformational challenges.
- Address product development, marketing, customer loyalty, innovation, operating models, and performance improvement.
- Typical workload: one to three concurrent projects, each approximately eight to sixteen weeks.
