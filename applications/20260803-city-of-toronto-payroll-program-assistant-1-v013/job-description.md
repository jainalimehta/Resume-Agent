Under the direction of the Manager, Workforce Planning & Development and reporting to the Supervisor, Operations Support PH, the Payroll Program Assistant 1 will perform a variety of payroll, clerical and administrative functions supporting Toronto Public Health.

## Major Responsibilities

- Directs and coordinates activities of Payroll Program Assistant 2 and administrative staff for manual entry and electronic payroll uploads into SAP, in line with complement/organization management and information reporting.
- Responds to management and employee inquiries and interprets collective agreements and corporate/service-area policies, procedures, guidelines, and directives for complement management and reporting.
- Updates, maintains, and monitors SAP with Pension, Payroll & Employee Benefits, People & Equity, Human Resources Organizational Management, and divisional stakeholders.
- Monitors manual and electronic payroll transactions/events in SAP Payroll and SuccessFactors and ensures accurate reflection in payroll reporting and HRIS systems.
- Monitors and verifies reconciliation reports for absenteeism, sick, vacation, and lieu banks.
- Participates in payroll and HR modernization initiatives, transformation, emergency events, and special projects.
- Coordinates internal projects and process reviews to improve services, standardize practices, and implement changes.
- Organizes and conducts ad hoc payroll-process inquiries and audits using electronic programs and hard-copy documents.
- Prepares, coordinates, analyzes, and maintains unit-budget administration, salary, and benefits information.
- Investigates problems, retrieves background information, and recommends actions to resolve discrepancies.
- Liaises with Toronto Public Health stakeholders, PPEB, People & Equity, and Technology Services Division.
- Prepares, reviews, and analyzes SAP and Labour Distribution Reports using complex tables, formatting, and pivot-table rules.
- Runs monthly Payroll Information Reports and ad hoc SAP/HRIS reports.
- Develops detailed organizational charts for assigned client portfolios.
- Oversees and monitors audits of payroll reports, adjustments, and staffing changes in SuccessFactors and SAP.
- Prepares documentation, reports, spreadsheets, correspondence, and complement/organization-structure information.

## Key Qualifications

- Considerable experience with payroll and HR database systems/software, including SAP, SuccessFactors, Document Direct, and SAP Business Warehouse, specifically in organizational and position management.
- Considerable experience applying union collective agreements, personnel policies, procedures, practices, and related legislation.
- Considerable customer-service experience across telephone, email, instant messaging, and Microsoft Teams.
- Considerable experience with Outlook and Microsoft Office, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, for complex reports, pivot tables, spreadsheets, correspondence, and data summaries.
- Experience directing work, delegating, training staff, and meeting tight deadlines in a high-volume environment.
- Knowledge of Employment Standards, Canada Pension Plan, Revenue Canada regulations, Employment Insurance, and WSIB legislation as applied to payroll.
- Analytical and problem-solving skills and ability to work independently.
- Productive relationship-building with HR staff, clients, and employees.
- Ability to work effectively in a team and respond professionally to colleagues.
- Ability to prepare accurate records and reports and maintain manual/computerized filing systems.
- Judgment and discretion with confidential matters.
- Ability to follow directions accurately with attention to detail.
- Clear written and verbal communication with all staff levels, the public, and other divisions.
- Ability to produce detailed statistical data and reports.
