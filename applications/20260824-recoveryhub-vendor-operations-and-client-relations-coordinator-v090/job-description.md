# Vendor Operations and Client Relations Coordinator

**Brand:** RecoveryHub  
**Employer:** CanCap Management Inc. / CanCap Group  
**Location:** 2680 Skymark Avenue, Mississauga, Ontario L4W 5L6  
**Work arrangement:** Hybrid; 50% in office and 50% working from home, subject to business needs  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Salary:** $47,500--$55,000 per year

At RecoveryHub, we are committed to excellence in repossession and insolvency management. Our business relies on a high-performing external network of asset recovery and remarketing partners. We are looking for an ambitious, highly communicative Vendor Operations and Client Relations Coordinator to help manage, support, and grow our bailiff and vendor network.

This is a high-impact, entry-level role designed for a dynamic new graduate who possesses strong emotional intelligence, sharp problem-solving skills, and a desire to build a career in operations, vendor management, and client relations.

## What You'll Do

As a Vendor Operations Coordinator, you will serve as the primary operational bridge between RecoveryHub and our field vendors/bailiff network. You will handle difficult conversations, build performance tools, and ensure operational excellence.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Bailiff and Vendor Relationship Management:** Build strong, trust-based rapport with bailiff and recovery partners. Confidently navigate challenging conversations, field vendor complaints or concerns with tact, and directly communicate company directives and operational issues.
- **Performance Tracking and Scorecard Development:** Construct, maintain, and analyze vendor performance scorecards to evaluate key SLAs, efficiency, and service standards.
- **Risk Awareness and Smart Escalation:** Exercise quick critical thinking and sound judgment to assess the risk level of conversations or issues, knowing when to resolve matters independently and when to engage senior leadership.
- **Contract Review and Analysis — Career Growth:** Learn to review incoming vendor contracts, evaluate terms, and provide initial internal feedback and risk assessments.
- **Client Presentations — Career Growth:** Work alongside leadership to develop and eventually deliver operational presentations to key clients.
- **Compliance and Operational Support:** Assist in managing annual compliance reviews and risk-mitigation efforts within the repossession and remarketing landscape.

## Who You Are

RecoveryHub is looking for a sharp, adaptable self-starter who thrives in fast-paced environments and is not afraid of complex communication challenges.

## Qualifications and Skills

- Recent graduate with a bachelor's degree in Business, Communications, Criminology/Law, Finance, or a related field.
- Zero to two years of professional experience; high-potential new graduates are strongly encouraged to apply.
- Superior verbal and written communication skills; comfortable speaking professionally with diverse personalities, handling conflict calmly, and asserting expectations clearly.
- Ability to collect operational data, build structured performance scorecards, and turn numbers into clear takeaways.
- Sound judgment: able to read between the lines, weigh risks, and identify when to escalate complex issues to management.
- Eager to learn contract mechanics, presentation delivery, and industry regulations.

## Working Conditions and Requirements

- Hybrid role requiring 50% of time in the office and 50% working from home, subject to business needs.
- Employment is subject to successful completion of a criminal background check and work-experience validation.

## Why Join RecoveryHub

RecoveryHub's team is driven by innovation, compliance, and operational efficiency. This role provides an opportunity for a new graduate to gain hands-on experience in vendor negotiations, performance analytics, contract analysis, and client delivery.

Adding to the team is one of the most important steps in the business. RecoveryHub asks applicants to be purposeful and thoughtful and explain how their background, skills, and communication strengths fit the role.
