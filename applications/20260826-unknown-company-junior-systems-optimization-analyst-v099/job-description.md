# Junior Systems Optimization Analyst

**Company:** Unknown company (posting category: Warehousing & Distribution)  
**Location:** Scarborough, Ontario, Canada  
**Employment:** Full-time; posting labels the level as Mid Level while describing an early-career role

## Role Summary

The Junior Systems Optimization Analyst works at the intersection of systems, operations, and data. The role is intended for an early-career candidate ready for ownership and operational impact. The analyst works cross-functionally to understand end-to-end operations, identify gaps, and develop practical solutions improving efficiency, accuracy, and scalability. Success requires systems thinking, curiosity about operations, and the ability to translate business needs into scalable solutions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Manage and support data-entry and reporting workflows across operational systems.
- Analyze and evaluate reporting systems, data pipelines, and workflows.
- Research, design, configure, and maintain automated reporting and operational processes.
- Monitor data integrity and system outputs for accuracy and reliability.
- Troubleshoot system issues and implement sustainable fixes instead of quick patches.
- Identify workflow gaps and recommend process and system enhancements.
- Assist in building structured performance-reporting frameworks tied to business metrics.
- Develop and maintain clear SOPs and documentation for systems and processes.
- Support maintenance, updates, and improvements of internal tools.
- Collaborate cross-functionally to understand needs and deliver holistic solutions.
- Maintain strict data and systems confidentiality and integrity.

## Technical Requirements

### Core Tools and Technologies

- MS Access VBA for RMA-system support.
- SQL development, querying, and automation scripts.
- Power BI, including DAX, dashboards, and reporting.
- Power Automate workflows integrating SharePoint and SQL.
- Power Apps for operational tools such as pick-and-pack systems.

### Programming and Development

- Working knowledge of C# and/or Python.
- Experience with rapid-development tools such as Replit.

### Emerging Technologies

- Practical understanding of AI tools, large language models, and automation agents.
- Ability to identify opportunities to apply AI to improve workflows.

## Soft Skills and Mindset

- Systems thinking across connected teams and processes.
- Business awareness before solution design.
- Data-driven validation of assumptions and decisions.
- Attention to accuracy, consistency, and data integrity.
- Ownership of outcomes.
- Root-cause problem solving and scalable fixes.
- User-focused solution design.
- Adaptability and action orientation in a fast-moving environment.
- Clear documentation and collaboration with technical and non-technical teams.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Familiarity with SharePoint workflows and database integrations.
- Exposure to Power Platform, Zapier, or similar automation tools and AI implementations.
- Experience in a lean or process-driven organization.
- Basic understanding of reporting frameworks and performance metrics.
