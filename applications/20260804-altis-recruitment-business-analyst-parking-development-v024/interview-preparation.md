## Analytical Workflow

1. Clarify the decision, stakeholder, time period, unit of analysis, and success measure.
2. Identify source systems, owners, refresh timing, definitions, access restrictions, and known limitations.
3. Profile completeness, validity, consistency, uniqueness, timeliness, and referential integrity.
4. Reconcile totals and exceptions against a trusted source before interpreting trends.
5. Segment by relevant dimensions such as location, time, channel, customer type, or equipment.
6. Compare actuals with targets, baselines, prior periods, and operational context.
7. Distinguish correlation from causation and document assumptions and limitations.
8. Present the finding, operational implication, recommendation, risk, owner, and next measure.
