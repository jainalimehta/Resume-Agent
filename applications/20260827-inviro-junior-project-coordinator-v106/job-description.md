# Junior Project Coordinator — Inviro Engineered Systems

**Location:** Scarborough, ON, Canada  
**Employment:** Full Time, On Site  
**Seniority shown in posting:** Experienced

INVIRO is seeking a motivated and passionate individual to join our growing team. As an employee of one of Canada’s leading Mechanical/Electrical engineering and sustainability consulting firms, you will be able to experience the thrills of working with a close-knit team on multiple challenging projects in the ICI sector.

We pride ourselves in enabling our employees to better themselves and provide them with opportunities to grow their skills and abilities. Additionally, we have developed and maintained a positive/nurturing company culture. All of this comes from our desire to see our employees succeed. If you’re ready to take the next step in your career, we’re excited to bring you onto our team.

## Duties and Responsibilities

- Support Project Managers in coordinating multidisciplinary mechanical, electrical, and building engineering projects from initiation through completion.
- Assist in preparing and maintaining project schedules, work plans, budgets, and project deliverables to help ensure projects are completed on time and within scope.
- Assist with the review, tracking, and administration of change notices and change orders, including coordinating technical input and monitoring status.
- Prepare and maintain project documentation including proposals, meeting minutes, progress reports, project correspondence, and close-out documentation.
- Coordinate communication between internal engineering teams, clients, consultants, contractors, and suppliers to facilitate effective project execution.
- Track project milestones, deliverables, and action items, following up with team members to ensure deadlines are met.
- Maintain organized project files, drawings, specifications, contracts, and documentation in accordance with company quality management procedures.
- Assist with procurement activities, equipment tracking, submittal reviews, and shop drawing coordination.
- Schedule project meetings, prepare agendas, record meeting minutes, and distribute action items.
- Assist in monitoring project risks and escalating issues to Project Managers when appropriate.
- Ensure project activities comply with company standards, client requirements, engineering practices, and health and safety policies.
- Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives by identifying opportunities to improve project coordination processes and documentation.
- Build positive working relationships with internal teams while providing professional and responsive client service.
- Perform other duties as assigned by Project Managers or senior leadership.

## Qualifications and Requirements

- Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Project Management, Engineering, Construction Management, or a related discipline.
- 0–2 years of experience in project coordination, project administration, engineering, construction, or a related professional services environment. Co-op, internship, or relevant academic project experience will also be considered.
- Basic understanding of project management principles, scheduling, budgeting, and project documentation.
- Strong proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, including Excel, Word, Outlook, PowerPoint, and SharePoint.
- Familiarity with project management software such as Microsoft Project, Procore, Deltek, Asana, or similar platforms is considered an asset.
- Exposure to consulting engineering, construction, or commercial building projects is considered an asset.
- Familiarity with AutoCAD, Revit, Bluebeam, or construction document management software is considered an asset.
- CAPM certification or willingness to pursue project management certification is considered an asset.
- Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills with the ability to collaborate effectively with internal teams and external stakeholders.
- Strong attention to detail, problem-solving skills, and a proactive approach to learning.
- Ability to work independently while contributing effectively within a collaborative team environment.
- Demonstrated professionalism, initiative, adaptability, and a commitment to delivering high-quality work and excellent customer service.
- Commitment to maintaining health and safety standards and company quality procedures.
