## Front-Line Enquiry Framework

1. Listen carefully and confirm the student's question.
2. Verify identity when accessing protected records.
3. Determine whether the request is within the role's authority.
4. Consult the current approved source rather than relying on memory.
5. Answer clearly or make a warm referral to the responsible office.
6. Record required follow-up and protect confidential information.
7. Confirm next steps, ownership, and expected timing without promising an unverified outcome.

## Student in Distress

- Remain calm, private, respectful, and non-judgmental.
- Ask whether there is an immediate safety concern; follow emergency protocol when required.
- Do not diagnose, counsel beyond training, or promise confidentiality that policy does not allow.
- Refer or connect the student to the designated University service and notify the appropriate supervisor.
- Record only the minimum required information under University procedure.

Present this as a researched response framework, not previous professional experience.
