## Role focus

Provide front-line administrative support to the Department of Computer Science graduate program under the Associate Director, Graduate Academic Services.

## Responsibilities

- Act as first contact for general enquiries; answer within scope and redirect appropriately.
- Write routine documents and correspondence.
- Determine event and programming logistics.
- Handle confidential information and maintain records and files.
- Collate and organize data for reports.
- Provide basic support and referrals to individuals in distress.

## Essential qualifications

- Advanced College Diploma or equivalent education and experience.
- Two years of front-line administrative support to faculty and students in a graduate academic environment.
- Student-services information covering admissions, registration, course enrolment, degree requirements, awards, and final examinations.
- Website, newsletter, digital-platform, and student-handbook content maintenance.
- Original correspondence and logistics for outreach, events, workshops, and programming.
- Databases, filing systems, and data compilation from multiple sources for reports.
- Quercus, ROSI, ROSI Express, eMarks, or similar academic systems.
- Advanced Microsoft Office, database management, and content-management systems.
- Excellent communication, client service, tact, diplomacy, discretion, organization, detail, time management, initiative, judgment, prioritization, and teamwork.

## Assets

- Knowledge of Department of Computer Science graduate programs.
- University of Toronto School of Graduate Studies practices, procedures, and fee schedules.
