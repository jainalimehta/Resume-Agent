2. Objective, measurable outcomes, and explicit non-goals.

4. Sponsor, project lead, contributors, decision-makers, and consulted groups.
5. Work breakdown, milestones, resources, and target dates.
6. Governance, status cadence, approvals, escalation, and document repository.

## Risk, Issue, Action, and Decision Discipline

- **Risk:** A possible future event; record likelihood, impact, owner, trigger, and mitigation.
- **Issue:** A current problem; record impact, owner, resolution action, target date, and escalation.
- **Action:** A defined task; assign one accountable owner and a due date.
- **Decision:** Record the choice, decision-maker, date, rationale, and downstream effects.

Use version control, consistent naming, controlled access, and a single source of truth. Separate broad status information from restricted HR or investigation material.

## Confidentiality Scenario

If confidential staffing or workplace-investigation information is received:

1. Confirm the approved audience and purpose before distributing anything.
2. Store material only in the authorized restricted location.
3. Apply least-privilege access and avoid sensitive detail in general trackers or email subject lines.
4. Verify recipients and attachments before sending.
5. Escalate uncertainty to the project sponsor, privacy, legal, or appropriate HR authority.
6. Follow retention and records-management requirements.

Do not claim knowledge of U of T's internal system configuration; describe this as a general confidentiality approach subject to University policy.
