# Junior Resource Strategy Analyst

## Role Summary

The Junior Resource Strategy Analyst supports organizational and project resource planning within the Project Controls department by analyzing workforce demand, capacity, utilization, and staffing trends. The role works with project managers, discipline leaders, and Human Resources to develop forecasts, identify gaps, optimize workforce allocation, and provide data-driven insights for strategic business decisions.

## Planning and Forecasting

- Assist the lead in developing and maintaining short- and long-term resource forecasts based on project portfolios, business pipelines, and operational requirements.
- Analyze workforce demand and capacity to identify shortages, surpluses, and critical skill gaps.
- Support annual workforce planning and budgeting.
- Collaborate with project and functional managers to align staffing needs with business priorities.
- Support other assigned cost-control tasks within Project Controls.

## Analytics and Reporting

- Monitor resource utilization, availability, productivity, and workforce trends.
- Update the Bridget Bench system and create dashboards and reports using Power BI, EcoSys, or other reporting platforms.
- Assist the lead in providing insights and recommendations to improve workforce efficiency and resource deployment.
- Track KPIs related to staffing, utilization, and resource performance.

## Strategic Workforce Management

- Support talent acquisition and workforce-planning initiatives by identifying future staffing requirements.
- Analyze organizational capability needs and recommend strategies to address skill shortages.
- Assist leadership in evaluating resource scenarios and business-growth opportunities.
- Support succession-planning and workforce-development initiatives.

## Stakeholder Collaboration

- Work with Engineering, Project Management, and HR teams to align resource plans with business objectives.
- Participate in resource-review meetings and coordinate systematic data flow into Project Controls.
- Communicate resource risks, constraints, and opportunities to management.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Project Management, Human Resources, Data Analytics, or a related field; new graduates are welcome.
- Preferred experience in resource planning, project controls, workforce planning, business analysis, project management, or related disciplines.
- Engineering, construction, infrastructure, consulting, or another project-based environment is an asset.
- ERP systems, resource-management tools, and reporting platforms are assets.
- Power BI, Power Query, or similar analytics-tool experience.
- Project-controls knowledge, including cost management, scheduling, and earned-value management, is an asset.
- Strong analytical and data-interpretation skills.
- Advanced Microsoft Excel.
- Cross-functional collaboration.
- Ability to meet tight deadlines and respond to ad-hoc requests under constrained capacity.
- Problem-solving, critical thinking, communication, presentation, organization, and multitasking skills.
