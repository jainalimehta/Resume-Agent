## Project Framework

1. Define the problem, objective, scope, non-goals, deliverables, and success measures.
2. Identify sponsor, decision-makers, contributors, dependencies, and governance.
3. Build milestones, owners, status cadence, risk/issue log, action register, and decision log.
4. Track implementation, communications, readiness, changes, and escalations.
5. Close with outcomes, lessons learned, unresolved items, and recommended next steps.
