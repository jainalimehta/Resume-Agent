# Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) — Administrative Services Inventory

## Important messages

We are committed to providing an inclusive and barrier-free work environment, starting with the hiring process. If you need to be accommodated during any phase of the evaluation process, please use the Contact information below to request specialized accommodation. All information received in relation to accommodation will be kept confidential.

When you apply to this selection process, you are not applying for a specific job, but to an inventory for future vacancies. As positions become available, applicants who meet the qualifications may be contacted for further assessment.

In the context of the RCMP’s mandate, most positions are identified as onsite. Where feasible, as determined by operational requirements, flexible work arrangements or a hybrid telework model with a maximum of two days at home may be considered after an initial full-time in-office training period.

All communication relating to this process, including email correspondence and telephone conversations, may be used in the assessment of qualifications.

## Work environment and process intent

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) is Canada's national police service. The intent is to establish an inventory of partially or fully assessed candidates for current and future vacancies of various tenures in Central Region. Applications and assessments are reviewed on an ongoing basis. Volume-management strategies may include top-down selection, cut-off scores, or random selection.

## Essential education

The minimum standard is a secondary school diploma or an employer-approved alternative. Acceptable experience and/or training in a field relevant to positions in the AS classification is required when the education qualification is a secondary school diploma or approved alternative. Original proof of education will be requested. Education obtained outside Canada must be evaluated.

## Conditions of employment

Various levels of security clearance may apply. Canadian citizenship is required for Top Secret clearance under the Treasury Board Secretariat Directive on Security Screening effective January 6, 2025. A valid RCMP security clearance is required before appointment and throughout employment. Screening may include interviews, field investigation, criminal-activity checks, credit/financial verification, references, education, and employment verification.

## Other information and preference

The Public Service of Canada promotes employment equity. Applications may be modified until the applicant participates in assessment. Reference checks may be sought. Candidates may participate in the official language of their choice. Communication will primarily be by email, and candidates must maintain a valid address. Candidates outside the federal public service pay their own travel expenses.

Preference will be given first to veterans and then to Canadian citizens and permanent residents, except for positions in Nunavut where Nunavut Inuit are appointed first.

Only candidates selected for further consideration will be contacted.
