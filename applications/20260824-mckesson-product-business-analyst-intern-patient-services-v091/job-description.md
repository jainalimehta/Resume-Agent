Product Business Analyst Intern, Patient Services
McKesson Canada Specialty Health

Job Summary
McKesson Canada Specialty Health is seeking a highly motivated Product Business Analyst Intern to join our Product Innovation team. This is an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience supporting digital transformation initiatives that improve patient access to therapy and enhance the delivery of patient support programs.

As a Product Business Analyst Intern, you will collaborate with product owners, business stakeholders, and technology teams to help design, analyze, and improve digital solutions that support patients, healthcare providers, and internal operations. You will contribute to product discovery, requirements gathering, process analysis, and solution design while gaining exposure to agile product development, healthcare technology, and emerging AI capabilities.

This role is ideal for a student or recent graduate who is passionate about healthcare innovation, technology, problem-solving, and creating meaningful patient experiences.

What You'll Do
Support product managers and business analysts in gathering and documenting business requirements.
Assist in mapping business processes, identifying pain points, and recommending opportunities for improvement.
Participate in workshops, stakeholder interviews, and product discovery activities.
Help translate business needs into user stories, acceptance criteria, process flows, and other project artifacts.
Assist with testing activities, including coordination, test script writing during user acceptance testing and validation of business requirements.
Explore opportunities to improve workflows through automation, digital solutions, and AI-enabled capabilities.
Participate in agile ceremonies including sprint planning, backlog refinement, demos, and retrospectives.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams across business, product, operations, and technology.
What We're Looking For
Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Business, Health Sciences, Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to think critically and ask thoughtful questions.
Interest in healthcare, digital transformation, product management, and innovation.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Ability to organize information and manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
Curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies, tools, and methodologies.
Experience with Microsoft Office, including Excel and PowerPoint.
Familiarity with process mapping, business analysis, Agile, AI tools, Salesforce, healthcare technology, or design thinking is considered an asset but not required.
What You'll Learn
Digital product management and agile delivery methodologies.
Business analysis techniques within a complex healthcare environment.
Product discovery, requirements gathering, and user-centered design.
Healthcare technology platforms supporting patient support programs and specialty pharmacy.
Digital transformation and AI-driven process improvement.
Cross-functional collaboration with business, product, operations, and technology teams.
Key Characteristics for Success
The ideal candidate is:

Curious, proactive, and eager to learn.
Patient-centric and motivated to make a positive impact.
Comfortable navigating ambiguity and solving complex problems.
Detail-oriented while maintaining a big-picture perspective.
Collaborative and effective at building relationships across diverse teams.
Passionate about innovation, technology, and improving healthcare experiences.
