## Major Responsibilities

- Collaborates with team to implement detailed plans and recommends policies/procedures regarding program-specific requirements.
- Researches, collects and analyses data for reporting the effectiveness and efficiency of operations.
- Facilitates and acts as supportive management of project streams across business process re-engineering, data management, test management, interface management, reporting, and training/communications.
- Enables and supports project staff regarding future-state data requirements, analysis of existing City data requiring cleansing and mapping, and approaches to prevent inaccuracies in current and future-state solutions.
- Works with Divisions to drive data migration, cleansing, transformation, and loading to the future-state solution.
- Uses technology to analyse system data and metrics, review data processes, identify issues, and determine actions to resolve or escalate system and process improvements.
- Documents Divisional impacts based on the degree of change between current- and future-state processes.
- Leads documentation of assigned work-package deliverables and works with implementation vendors.
- Collaborates with internal and external customers to elicit requirements for future-state solutions and works with consultants, staff, and partners on planning, feasibility studies, cost-benefit analysis, and future-state design.
- Collaborates with Technology Services Division to deliver the future-state solution.
- Supports cost-effective solutions, workarounds, and contingency plans to remove obstacles affecting milestones.
- Works on end-to-end project testing including unit, functional, quality assurance, integration, parallel, regression, and cutover tests.
- Creates and maintains test strategies, plans, cases, and scripts; executes tests across development, quality assurance, and pre-production landscapes.
- Troubleshoots test cases and monitors and reports test progress with Technology Services Division.
- Provides daily/weekly milestone documentation and reports issues, risks, and decisions using project-management standards.
- Prepares decision documents with option analysis, cost-benefit analysis, and recommendations for Project Sponsors.
- Supports organizational change-management strategies, cutover tasks, delivery, and production end-user readiness.
- Conducts reporting analysis through requirements gathering and future reporting capabilities.
- Prepares reports, flowcharts, briefing notes, and presentations.

## Key Qualifications

- Post-secondary education in computer science or a related discipline, or equivalent education/experience, with a strong focus on Materials Management and Logistics environments (SAP MM, LE-WM, and EWM).
- Considerable experience with financial/data systems specific to SAP data management, analytics, and reporting, with expertise in SAP ECC and S/4HANA transformations.
- Extensive experience creating business and system processes and developing administrative work procedures and systems.
- Considerable experience with financial systems such as SAP ECC and S/4HANA, project-management tools, and Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Access, PowerPoint) for reports, databases, spreadsheets, and presentations.
- Experience writing reports, briefings, presentations, and policy papers for senior management and providing strategic advice on sensitive issues.
- Ability to analyse and extract large volumes of statistical and material data, interpret reports and research materials, and prepare and present complex information for decision-making.
- Highly developed customer service, interpersonal, presentation, and communication skills.
- Excellent research, analytical, problem-solving, conflict-resolution, and strategic-thinking skills.
- Ability to handle multiple tasks under pressure and tight deadlines while maintaining accuracy and quality.
- Sound judgment and discretion with sensitive or confidential matters.
