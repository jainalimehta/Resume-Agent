# Data Analyst - Global Compliance Data Analytics (Contract)

Requisition ID: 270323

Location: Toronto, Ontario, Canada

## Purpose

The Data Analyst contributes to the team’s success and works with business analysts, data scientists, and data engineers to address business opportunities. The role understands business needs and requirements to support and enhance governance and analytics within Compliance.

## What You'll Do

- Contribute to identifying and registering data elements, Critical Data Elements, and associated source and target systems.
- Support documentation and validation of Data Flow Diagrams, Data Lineage, and Source-to-Target Mapping.
- Contribute to business and technical Data Quality rules for Critical Data Elements.
- Execute data profiling, data-quality assessment, issue root-cause analysis, investigation, and remediation.
- Conduct Quality Assurance Testing, Business Acceptance Testing, and Post Implementation Validation.
- Champion a customer-focused culture and leverage Bank relationships, systems, and knowledge.
- Provide technical and analytical expertise in designing and implementing data pipelines.
- Plan and facilitate discussions with business clients to gather business and data-source requirements and use cases supporting Data Governance, Advanced Analytics, and Modeling.
- Create dashboards and data features for governance, modeling, reporting, and advanced analytics.
- Build and maintain data-quality reporting, data-lineage, Source-to-Target Mapping, and ETL documentation.
- Understand Bank strategies, policies, risk management, IT processes, risk appetite, and risk culture.
- Pursue efficient operations in accordance with Scotiabank values, the Code of Conduct, Global Sales Principles, and controls for operational, compliance, and conduct risk.
- Champion a high-performance and inclusive work environment.

## What You Bring

- University degree or college diploma in a relevant STEM discipline, Economics, Finance, or a related field.
- Familiarity with data governance, data management, and data-quality concepts including Critical Data Elements, Authoritative Data Sources, Master and Reference Data, Data Lineage, Metadata, Data Flows, and Data Issues Management.
- Two or more years cleaning, transforming, and visualizing datasets from formats such as unstructured logs, XML, JSON, and flat files.
- Hands-on programming experience and advanced SQL.
- Experience with Agile methodologies and visualization tools such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Excellent communication and strong customer focus.
- Experience with statistical analysis and machine learning libraries in R, Matlab, or Python.
- Collibra or similar data-governance tooling is an asset.

Candidates must apply directly online. Scotiabank is committed to inclusion and accessibility throughout recruitment.
