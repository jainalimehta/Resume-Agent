## Role focus

Implement, configure, govern, administer, support, and continuously optimize the Project Management Information System (PMIS). Liaise among the PMO, business stakeholders, IT, finance, and project managers to support planning, cost control, reporting, governance, training, and adoption.

## Responsibilities

- Administer PMIS configuration, performance, security, availability, accounts, access, roles, and permissions.
- Gather, document, validate, and translate business requirements into enhancements, reporting solutions, and functional specifications.
- Support integrations with ERP, finance, HR, and enterprise systems.
- Establish data standards, naming conventions, coding structures, governance controls, and data-integrity monitoring.
- Provide user support, training materials, documentation, and best-practice guidance.
- Design dashboards, KPIs, metrics, and reports for schedule, cost, earned value, forecasting, and risk.
- Analyze workflows and recommend system-enabled process improvements aligned with project-management methods.

## Essential qualifications

- Related bachelor's degree or equivalent.
- Four years administering or supporting PMIS or enterprise project-management platforms.
- Construction, engineering, infrastructure, capital-project delivery, and ERP/financial integration experience.
- Power BI or Tableau; PMIS such as Primavera P6, MS Project Online/Server, Kahua, e-Builder, or similar.
- Project management knowledge covering WBS, scheduling, cost control, procurement, risk, contracts, and resource planning.
- Requirements translation, analytical problem solving, stakeholder facilitation, communication, accuracy, and competing priorities.
