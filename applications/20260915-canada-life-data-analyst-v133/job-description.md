# Data Analyst — Canada Life

**Employment:** Permanent, full time  
**Team:** Canada Operations & Business Enablement, Data Analytics and Reporting  
**Locations:** Toronto, London, or Winnipeg  
**Work model:** Hybrid, three days per week in office  
**Salary:** CAD $65,800–$109,700

As a Data Analyst, you will support Canada Operations and Business Enablement teams with the development, implementation, and maintenance of analytics solutions ranging from descriptive and diagnostic to predictive. The Analyst, Data & Analytics supports decision making, monitors business performance, and provides actionable insights using analytics and statistical techniques. The role assists in modelling, analysing, and predicting business processes and translates business requirements into data, reporting, and analytics projects throughout their lifecycle.

## What You Will Do

- Translate business needs into data requirements; collect, integrate, clean, explore, model, and analyze large datasets; visualize results and present to business leaders.
- Build, implement, and maintain data models, custom reports, and dashboards.
- Work with large datasets that are inconsistent, unclean, or not fully modelled.
- Communicate effectively and demonstrate critical thinking and business acumen.
- Define, implement, and monitor metrics and KPIs aligned with short- and long-term business objectives.
- Build effective working relationships with business partners.
- Work with senior leaders to understand business drivers and enable data-based decisions.
- Support projects throughout the analytics lifecycle.
- Present insights and recommendations in a visual and simplified manner.

## What You Will Bring

- Reliability Status Security Clearance, completed after an offer as a condition of employment.
- One to three years of relevant experience and a bachelor's degree in a related field, or an equivalent combination.
- Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate (PL-300) preferred.
- Demonstrated experience with SQL, Python, SQL Server, Azure, Databricks, and Power BI, including interactive dashboards.
- Ability to write efficient SQL and Python queries to extract, integrate, and transform data from multiple sources and automate data pipelines.
- Proficiency in Power BI, data modelling, Power Query, and DAX.
- Experience manipulating raw, large datasets.
- Knowledge of data analytics and statistical modelling.
- Strong communication and presentation skills.
- Ability to learn quickly, work independently, and manage multiple priorities.
- Financial-services experience is an asset.
