# Generalist Expert — AI Training and Evaluation

**Company:** Mercor  
**Location:** United States or Canada; fully remote  
**Engagement:** Independent contractor

## Overview

Mercor is seeking United States- or Canada-based generalist experts to help train and evaluate frontier AI models. Contributors review everyday professional content, including documents, slides, spreadsheets, and other written materials, judging quality, accuracy, clarity, and completeness. Feedback shapes how AI systems reason about and produce real-world work products.

The opportunity suits detail-oriented generalists who can move across formats and subject areas. Deep specialization is not required; strong judgment, careful reading, and clear written reasoning matter most.

## What You Will Do

- Review and evaluate documents, slides, spreadsheets, and similar materials for quality and correctness.
- Provide clear, well-reasoned written feedback and ratings.
- Compare and rank AI-generated outputs against defined criteria.
- Flag errors, inconsistencies, and gaps in reasoning or formatting.

## What Mercor Is Looking For

- Bachelor's degree at minimum.
- Based in the United States or Canada.
- Excellent reading comprehension and written communication in English.
- Strong attention to detail and sound judgment across varied subject matter.
- Comfort working independently across common productivity tools, including documents, slides, and spreadsheets.
- No prior AI or machine-learning experience required; guidelines and context will be provided.

Mercor considers qualified applicants without regard to legally protected characteristics and provides reasonable accommodations upon request.

## Contract and Payment Terms

- Independent-contractor engagement.
- Fully remote work completed on the contractor's own schedule.
- Projects can be extended, shortened, or concluded early depending on project needs and performance.
- Work will not involve confidential or proprietary information from another employer, client, or institution.
- Weekly payments through Stripe or Wise based on services rendered.
- Mercor cannot support H1-B or STEM OPT candidates for this opportunity.
